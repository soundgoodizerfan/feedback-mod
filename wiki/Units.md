# Units

Every figure in Feedback has a unit, and the same symbol is used on screens, tooltips, Jade and JEI. This page says what each one measures. The pages named under each heading give the figures themselves.

## Temperature (Tu)

**Tu** is temperature, and nothing else: a piece at 900 Tu has more glow than one at 800 Tu, not more of anything. Without an instrument you only see words for it. A [Thermometer](Instruments.md#thermometer) gives the figure. The scale is on [Heat](Heat.md#temperature-tu).

## Load and speed (Su, RPM)

**Su** is rotational load and supply. Every source of turning power carries some, and every machine adds load to the line it sits on. **RPM** is how fast a shaft turns, in revolutions per minute. See [Rotation](Rotation.md#power-su).

## Blows (St, Fu)

**St** is the strength of one blow: how hard a hammer or press lands. A metal's hardness is a strength too, and a blow weaker than it does nothing. **Fu** is the work a piece has taken so far, counted up blow by blow until the step is done. See [Metalworking](Metalworking.md#work-strength-and-hardness).

## Energy (Work)

**Work** is energy. The heat a boiler spends on steam, a bullet's energy in flight and the force of a stagger or a parry are all counted in Work. See [Boiler](Boiler.md), [Guns](Guns.md) and [Combat](Combat.md).

## Mass (Mu)

**Mu** is mass. A heavier head hits harder and swings slower, and the mass a body carries sets how long it staggers. A [Balance Scale](Instruments.md#balance-scale) reads it. Materials carry a density, mass per mB, on [Materials](Materials.md).

## Volume (mB)

**mB** is a millibucket, the volume of fluid and of air. A bucket holds 1000 mB, as in vanilla. Molten metal is measured in mB too: an ingot's worth is 144 mB and a block's 1296 mB. Tanks, boilers, engines and the [Pressure Vessel](Pressure_Vessel.md) all show their contents in mB.

## Amount (Qu)

**Qu** is an amount of stuff. One Qu is the amount of metal in a nugget, so an ingot is 9 Qu and a block 81. Molten, 1 Qu of metal is 16 mB. Equal Qu of different substances hold the same amount of stuff, while their masses differ (as with the mole before 2019, no atoms are meant). A concentration is Qu per mB. The [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md) moves metal in Qu, and a food's sugar, vitamins, live germs and toxin are in Qu too ([Food](Food.md)).

## Pressure (Pu)

**Pu** is pressure: how full a sealed space is of air or steam. Pump more in over the same volume and the number climbs. The [Boiler](Boiler.md) and the [Pressure Vessel](Pressure_Vessel.md) each give the figures and what happens past the safe ones.

## Electricity (V, A)

**V** is voltage, how hard the current is pushed, and **A** is amps, how much flows. Voltage comes in bands: LV, MV, HV and UHV. See [Electricity](Electricity.md#volts-and-amps).

## Radiation (Ru)

**Ru** is a dose rate: how much radiation a body takes each second. See [Radiation](Radiation.md).

## Experience (XP)

**XP** is experience points, counted as in vanilla. A container's level is its XP over its capacity. See [Experience](Experience.md).
