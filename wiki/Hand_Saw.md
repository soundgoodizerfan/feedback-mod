# Hand Saw

A modular hand tool that goes in the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md)'s saw slot. The stonecutter does nothing without one.

## Crafting

```
P P
T
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate and T is a Stick.

## Making a better one

Shape a **Saw Blade** (two cells of plate) at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md), then put it with a handle (stick, rod or bone) and assemble, as described in [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md#putting-one-together). The blade's material sets how long the saw lasts.

## Using it

Put the saw in the saw slot of the Stonecutter. Every act there costs it a point of wear: shaping a part, breaking stock down, or a cut on the Cut tab (stone cuts, and wood cuts when a plank or log is in the center). With the slot empty the screen reads "Put a hand saw in the saw slot."
