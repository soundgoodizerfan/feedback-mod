# Thermometer

Turns "glowing orange" into a number.

## Crafting

```
G
F
P
```

G is a Glass Pane, F is [Copper Foil](Mechanical_Hammer.md) and P is a [Copper Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md).

## Using it

**Carry it anywhere in your inventory.** You don't need to hold it.

- Hot **items** show a temperature in Tu on their tooltip instead of a colour word.
- **Crucibles**, cable, the Improved Furnace and other open blocks show a figure in Jade.

**Right-click** any hot block, even a **sealed** furnace, to take one reading through the door. Sealed furnaces can't be *watched* (Jade just says *"Sealed - cannot be measured"*), but you can always check them by hand.

## It's rough

The thermometer reads in steps of **25 Tu**. Steel's window is 60 Tu wide, so it's about two steps: you'll know you're in it but not exactly where. It's still enough to make steel reliably by hand: heat, check, wait, check, adjust.

For finer readings, and readings a [Controller](Controller.md) can use, bolt a [Temperature Sensor](Temperature_Sensor.md) onto the vessel.

## Why it matters

Before the thermometer, "hot" is all you've got. Nothing is locked behind it; you can make steel without one if you're lucky. What it gives you is the ability to do it on purpose.
