# Netherite

The hardest metal, made from Nether scrap or synthesized from powders. Both routes end the same way: a **Cast Netherite Ingot**, soul-quenched into a netherite ingot.

## Getting the scrap

- **Ancient debris** is mined as in vanilla. Hardness of at least 40 digs it ([Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md)).
- **Worn Netherite Bits** drop from **Bastion Rubble**, a rare block in the Nether (1 to 3 per block), and from piglins and piglin brutes killed by a player (15% of kills, 1 or 2 bits).
- Vanilla's smelting of debris into scrap, the scrap-and-gold recipe and the smithing-table upgrades do not exist here. See [Ancient Debris](Ancient_Debris.md).

## Melting

Ancient debris, netherite scrap, Worn Netherite Bits, netherite ingots, netherite blocks and Cast Netherite Ingots all melt into **Molten Cast Netherite** at **2600 Tu**. That is hotter than any fire; use an [Arc Furnace](Arc_Furnace.md). Its hearth wears above 2,200 Tu, so fit a [Refractory Lining](Refractory_Lining.md).

| Item | Gives |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient debris | 36 mB |
| Netherite scrap | 36 mB |
| Worn Netherite Bits | 48 mB |
| Cast Netherite Ingot | 144 mB |

Netherite gear melts down the same way, so existing pieces return as metal.

Cast the melt in a [Casting Basin](Casting_Basin.md) to a **Cast Netherite Ingot**. It is a magnetic, fireproof alloy of hardness **26**, short of netherite's 40.

## Soul quenching

A Cast Netherite Ingot becomes netherite only by quenching in soul oil.

1. Fill a tank with [soul oil](Oil.md#soul-oil) (at least 50 mB).
2. Heat the Cast Netherite Ingot to **1200 Tu** or more.
3. Right-click the tank with the hot ingot in hand.

The ingot comes out as a vanilla **netherite ingot** (hardness **40**) and the tank loses 50 mB of soul oil. A cooler ingot is not transformed. Carry hot pieces with [tongs or gloves](Hazards.md#tongs).

## Synthesis

Netherite can be made without the Nether's scrap.

1. Mix **Netherite Powder** in a [Mixer](Mixer.md): 55 to 65% cermet, 20 to 30% tungsten and 10 to 20% cobalt, with the drum at 600 Tu or under.
2. Press the powder into a **Netherite Green Compact** in the [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md#pressing-powder).
3. Sinter the compact at **1500 to 1700 Tu** under **50 mB of nitrogen**. The Kiln stops short of this heat; the [Arc Hearth](Arc_Furnace.md#sintering-under-gas) holds it. It makes a Cast Netherite Ingot. Hotter than 1700 Tu, or without the nitrogen, it leaves Porous Sinter.
4. Soul-quench the ingot, as above.
