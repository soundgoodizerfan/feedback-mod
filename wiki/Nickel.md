# Nickel

Nickel is a magnetic, tough, corrosion-resistant metal. It is the cathode of the iron and nickel battery and a partner in stainless steel, nichrome and several magnetic alloys. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

Its ore is pentlandite (60% nickel), in the Overworld from y -32 to 40 in the mafic complex and in the hot Nether belt from y 10 to 117 ([Ores](Ores.md)). Roast the concentrate, leach it with sulfuric acid in a [Reactor](Reactor.md) to Nickel Sulfate Liquor, then electrowin it: the [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md#plated-metal) plates 1 Nickel Nugget per 16 mB of liquor ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#roasting-sulfides)). The leach also gives 2 cobalt nuggets and, rarely, a platinum or palladium nugget.

## Figures

| Figure | Nickel |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 9 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 8.91 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1455 |
| Toughness | 100 |
| Yield strength (St) | 3.5 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 13.4 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0227 |
| Conductivity | 0.22 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.9 |

Nickel is **magnetic** below 355 Tu, so an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) pulls it. It is as hard as iron but much weaker, very tough, and it resists corrosion well enough that it takes no wear in a battery's electrolyte ([Battery](Battery.md#the-electrolyte)).

## What it is used in

- **Batteries.** Nickel is the cathode of the Edison cell with an iron anode, which keeps most of its charge in the cold ([Battery](Battery.md)).
- **Stainless steel.** 6 to 10% of the melt ([Stainless Steel](Stainless_Steel.md)).
- **Alloys.** Nichrome with chromium, constantan and cupronickel with copper, invar with iron, alnico, and the binder of cermet ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys), [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 3 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
