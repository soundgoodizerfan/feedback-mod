# Bearing

A [Shaft](Shaft.md) with a proper bearing on it. It does exactly what a shaft does, but loses much less power to friction: **0.04 Su per RPM** against a shaft's 0.1.

## Crafting

```
I
S
I
```

I is a Wrought Iron Plate and S is a Shaft. Makes **two**.

## Using it

Use it anywhere you'd use a shaft. Friction adds up on long runs and fast lines, which is where it helps.

Bearings are also an ingredient in most of the shaft-driven workshop machines: the [Wire Drawer](Wire_Drawer.md), [Rolling Mill](Rolling_Mill.md), [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md), [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md), [Lathe](Lathe.md) and [Grinding Wheel](Grinding_Wheel.md).

> Not to be confused with the [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md), which turns a built piece, or the [joint voxels](Vehicles.md#joints) of a micro-block vehicle.
