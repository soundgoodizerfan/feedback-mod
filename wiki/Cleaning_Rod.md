# Cleaning Rod

A steel rod with a rag at its tip. It pushes a round that lodged in a gun's bore back out (see [Squibs](Guns.md#squibs)).

## Crafting

```
W
R
```

W is white wool and R is a Steel Rod.

## Using it

Hold the rod in one hand and the gun in the other, then use the rod. The lodged round comes out (*"You push the round out of the bore."*) and the rod is busy for about 3 seconds, a lost turn. With nothing lodged, using it does nothing.

## Misuse and hazards

Firing a lodged gun puts many times the round's pressure on the chamber, which damages the gun and hurts you ([Overpressure](Guns.md#overpressure), [Hazards](Hazards.md)). Clear the bore first.
