# Oscilloscope

Watches the current in a cable and shows it on a [Phosphor Screen](Phosphor_Screen.md).

## Crafting

Shapeless: a Galvanometer, a Wrought Iron Plate, Copper Wire and Ceramic.

## Using it

Put it **against a cable** and **against a screen**. It joins no line; it only reads. Every tenth of a second it samples the cable and drives the screen to match (four levels). **Right-click** it to see the last 16 samples as a row of bars, to the nearest 0.25 A, signed by the scope.

Above 4 A the trace sits pegged at the top, exact and wrong. A pegged screen is also at full brightness, which is what [burns it in](Phosphor_Screen.md#overrun). It has no use but to look; it changes nothing in the line.
