# Steel

Steel is iron with carbon: harder and stronger than iron, and the metal of boilers, engines, plates and most machines. It can be quench-hardened. It belongs to the Steel [age](Ages.md).

## Making it

Steel is not a mixture of melts. Put a **Wrought Iron Ingot** and **Charcoal** in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) and hold it between **1350 and 1410 Tu**; above 1470 Tu the batch spoils to Burnt Iron. Cast iron with charcoal gives steel too. The full path, with the failure points, is [Guide: Making Steel](Guide_Making_Steel.md).

Molten steel is the base of [stainless steel](Stainless_Steel.md), manganese steel and high-speed steel ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).

## Figures

| Figure | Steel |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 15 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 7.85 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1450 |
| Toughness | 50 |
| Yield strength (St) | 12 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 12 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0125 |
| Conductivity | 0.1 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.35 |

Steel only moves under the hammer between **900 and 1100 Tu**, so it has to be hot, and a fresh ingot burns bare hands: carry it with [tongs](Hazards.md#tongs). It is **magnetic** below 770 Tu. It **quench hardens**: [hardened steel](Guide_Heat_Treatment.md#hardening-quenching) is far harder, and [tempered steel](Guide_Heat_Treatment.md#tempering) is hard and tough again. Steel conducts poorly and rusts a little less than iron.

## What it is used in

- **Machine plates and rods.** Steel Plates and Rods go into the [Controller](Controller.md), the [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md), the [Arc Furnace](Arc_Furnace.md), [Induction](Induction.md) coils, the [Cryocooler](Cryocooler.md), the [Charger](Charger.md), the [Ballast Tank](Ballast_Tank.md), the [Control Rod Assembly](Control_Rod.md), the [Auto Engineer](Auto_Engineer.md) and the improved [furnaces](Furnaces.md).
- **Engines.** Steel pistons make a good all-round engine ([Engine](Engine.md)).
- **Couplings.** A steel sleeve carries a bearing's whole torque limit ([Coupling Sleeve](Coupling_Sleeve.md)).
- **Guns.** A steel chamber rates 8 Pu for a 9 mm bore and 3 mm wall, and a steel case holds 9 Pu ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber), [Ammunition](Ammunition.md#cases)).
- **Fuse wire.** Carries 1.6 A forever and blows above about 4.5 A ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 3 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
