# Gold

Gold is a very soft, very dense metal that never corrodes. It is the noblest metal in the [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md#the-liquid-picks-the-metal), and the best material for taking enchantment. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Gold Ore lies in the Overworld from y -64 to 32, again from y -64 to -48, and on badlands mesas from y 32 to 256, all in the porphyry belt. Nether Gold Ore lies in the Nether from y 10 to 117 ([Ores](Ores.md)). Gossan Rubble carries a little gold ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#stained-soil-as-ore)).
- **Melting.** Raw gold or crushed ore melts to molten gold in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md); the further the ore is processed, the more metal it gives.
- **Amalgam.** Mercury takes gold out of a concentrate at 10 to 60 Tu, and heating the amalgam to 360 to 600 Tu gives 12 gold nuggets back ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#gold-by-amalgam)).

## Figures

| Figure | Gold |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 2.5 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 19.3 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1064 |
| Toughness | 100 |
| Yield strength (St) | 1.5 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 14.2 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0791 |
| Conductivity | 0.7 |
| Corrosion resistance | 1 |

Gold is nearly three times as heavy as iron and very soft. A gold head hits hard and swings slowly, and a gold edge is too soft to mine iron ore and wears out fast ([Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md#what-the-parts-do)). Gold armor protects little but is heavy, with the knockback resistance of dense metal ([Armor](Armor.md)). A gold gun chamber rates only 1 Pu ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber)).

Gold takes enchantment best of any metal: it holds the most effects, and its levels are the strongest ([Enchanting Table](Enchanting_Table.md#how-strong-and-how-many)).

## What it is used in

- **Dosimeter.** Gold Foil, glass tubing and a wrought iron plate make the [Dosimeter](Instruments.md#dosimeter).
- **Fuse wire.** Carries 11.2 A forever and blows above about 13 A ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Alloys.** Electrum with silver and shakudo with copper ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
