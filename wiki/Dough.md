# Dough

Wheat and water, worked by hand into bread and flatbread. Dough is where the [Prep Table](Prep_Table.md)'s Knead comes in.

## Making dough

Put **three wheat and a water bucket** in the crafting grid, in any arrangement, for **three dough**. The bucket comes back empty. Vanilla bread (three wheat in a row) does not exist: bread is a dish made from dough, see [Foods](Foods.md#bread).

Raw dough is barely food. Bake it in a [Furnace, Smoker](Furnaces.md), [Skillet](Skillet.md) or [Stockpot](Stockpot.md), as any food: it cooks through into a **Loaf** (the same hunger and saturation as vanilla bread), and keep it on and it dries out and then chars like anything else.

## Kneading

Put one dough in the work slot of the Prepare tab and press **Knead**. No knife is needed. You work the dough with your hands:

- **Hold the left button to press.** Lower on screen is harder. Let go to rest.
- Pressing builds **gluten**, which is what makes dough smooth and good to eat, but it also **tires** the dough. Tense dough takes work badly, and only resting lets it relax. So the game is when to push and when to wait.
- You read the dough, not a number. It **smooths and turns glossy** as the gluten builds, **reddens and shakes** as it tenses, and **cracks** when it tears. A dry, unworked dough is flaky with crumbs.
- Press **Done** when you are satisfied (the time runs out on its own). The dough rests under a cloth while music plays for how it went, then the cloth lifts.

| Result | What it is | Loaf |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Glossy** | well worked, then rested | feeds more |
| **Good** | worked enough | as vanilla bread |
| **Crumbly** | not worked enough | feeds less |
| **Tough** | overworked and torn | feeds least |

Dough nobody kneaded bakes into a loaf a little under good (the unskilled floor): a plain batch off a machine is never refused, only worse. A glossy loaf is the best bread there is.

**How the material changes the game.** The dough itself sets the feel. *Water* makes it sticky: it lags your hand, oozes back slowly, and gluten builds slowly, while a drier dough follows your hand at once, bounces, and tears at a lower tension. *Fibre* tenses dough faster and rests it slower, so a fibrous dough wants shorter, lighter pushes. *Fat* tenderises: gluten builds slower, but the dough tires less. No dough is best everywhere; each is its own rhythm of push and wait.

## Flatbread

One dough in the crafting grid makes one **Flatbread Dough**. Baked like any food it becomes **Flatbread**, a sheet you can wrap a dish in: it is the container of the **Wrap** at the Prep Table. An unbaked flatbread dough still goes in as a container, but a raw one feeds almost nothing.
