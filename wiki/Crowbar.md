# Crowbar

A bent bar of wrought iron for prying fittings off a machine.

## Crafting

Two Wrought Iron Plates on a diagonal.

```
. P
P .
```

## Using it

Right-click a machine that carries fittings, such as a [Crucible](Crucible.md), a furnace (see [Furnaces](Furnaces.md)) or a cable.

- A sensor on the face you click comes off first.
- With none there, the last upgrade fitted comes off, such as a [Refractory Lining](Refractory_Lining.md). A [Controller](Controller.md) keeps a part its card needs.

The fitting drops as its item. Each prying wears the crowbar a little.
