# Optics

Lenses, fiber, the pyrometer and the telescope, all from glass.

## Lens

Grind a glass plate into a **lens**. Carry one and every reading of a length, a condition, a hardness or an ore gets a finer step. The clearer the glass, the finer: optical glass narrows a step to 0.65 of its size, silica glass to 0.8. A truth reading stays exact.

## Fiber

Draw a glass rod into **fiber**, a thread of glass.

## Pyrometer

A [Thermometer](Instruments.md#thermometer) with a lens in it. Right-click a vessel up to 24 blocks away to read its temperature in steps of 10 Tu, sealed furnace or not.

> **Recipe:** an optical glass lens over a [Photocell](Sensors.md) over a Wrought Iron Plate, in a column. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row, 1 screw ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)) and Electrical Treatise I: Cells and Static Charge ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Telescope

Two lenses and a tube make a [Telescope](Telescope.md): hold right click to look through it, magnified by the ratio of the lenses' focal lengths. Held in the other hand beside a [Camera](Camera.md), it is the camera's lens.

A pumped crystal makes a [Laser](Laser.md): a placed block that measures distance and cuts what its beam rests on.
