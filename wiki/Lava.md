# Lava

A vanilla fluid. Feedback keeps what it does to you and gives it a second job as a fire.

## What vanilla does

It burns and damages anything that touches it, destroys dropped items, lights flammable blocks, and a lava bucket is a long-burning furnace fuel.

## What Feedback does

Burning, damage, destroyed items and lit blocks all happen as in vanilla. A lava bucket is not a fuel: a [Firebox](Firebox.md#fuels) refuses it. Lava is a heat source instead, a **fire fixed at 1200 Tu** that never changes and cannot be blown hotter:

- **Under a vessel.** A [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [Boiler](Boiler.md) placed straight over lava climbs toward 1200 Tu and sits just under it forever. It is the steadiest heat source in the game and **too cool for steel** (1350 Tu). See [Heat](Heat.md#a-fire-is-a-temperature-not-an-amount).
- **Items that survive it.** An item, or a finished tool, made of stuff whose melting point (for what burns, its burning point) is above lava's 1200 Tu is fire resistant, as netherite gear is: it does not burn up in lava or fire. Iron, steel, tungsten, titanium, chromium and netherite qualify, and so does anything assembled only from such parts. Copper, gold, aluminum, tin, lead and zinc do not.
- **In your hand.** Right-click a lava source with a workpiece and it is heated to 1200 Tu on the spot. Crude and uncontrolled, but it needs no machine. A hot piece [hurts to hold](Hazards.md) without gloves or [tongs](Hazards.md#tongs).
