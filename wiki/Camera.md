# Camera

A hand-held camera that takes a small photograph of what you see. A [Telescope](Telescope.md) in the other hand is its lens.

## Crafting

```
L X
P P
```

L is an Optical Glass Lens, X is a [Photocell](Sensors.md) and P is a Wrought Iron Plate. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row, 1 screw ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)) and Electrical Treatise II: Coils and Magnets ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

**Hold right click** to expose and **let go** to take the photograph. Let go before half a second has passed and nothing is taken. Between photographs the camera needs about 2 seconds.

The photograph is a **Photograph** item holding a small picture of 64 by 48 dots. Right-click with it to open the picture. A Photograph that has taken nothing is blank.

## The lens

- **With a telescope in the other hand**, the telescope is the lens. Its [magnification](Telescope.md#how-it-works) crops the picture in, and its blur softens it. A telescope out of focus, or a steel tube in the cold, takes a soft picture.
- **With no telescope**, the camera is a pinhole: no zoom and a soft picture.
