# Iron Ore

Vanilla's iron ore and its raw iron, and what they make here. In Feedback they are the start of the [wrought iron](Making_Wrought_Iron.md) route, not a furnace recipe.

## What vanilla does

Iron Ore and Deepslate Iron Ore drop Raw Iron. Raw Iron, or the ore block itself, smelts to an Iron Ingot in a furnace or blast furnace.

## What Feedback does

The ores are found and mined as in vanilla and drop Raw Iron ([Ore Deposits](Ore_Deposits.md#mining-them)). Raw Iron and iron ore do not smelt to an ingot in any [furnace](Furnaces.md) or [crucible](Crucible.md). Instead:

- **With charcoal**, held in the bloom window, the ore becomes an **Iron Bloom** full of slag, which is hammered into a Wrought Iron Ingot. See [Making Wrought Iron](Making_Wrought_Iron.md).
- **Bare, or too hot**, the ore melts to **molten cast iron**, the start of [steel](Steel.md).

Raw iron also takes the [ore processing](Ore_Processing.md) stages like any other ore: crushing and separating it first gives more iron than melting the raw rock.

Feedback's own iron minerals (hematite, magnetite, limonite) follow the same route ([Materials](Materials.md#ore-minerals)).
