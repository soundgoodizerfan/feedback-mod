# Smithing Table

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps the block and changes what it is for.

## In vanilla

It upgrades diamond gear to netherite and applies armor trims, using smithing templates.

## In Feedback

That use is gone in its old form (there is no smithing screen of the old kind). The Smithing Table is where **metal parts** are shaped and forged, played as the forging rhythm game, with a [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) in the hammer slot. Stock must be metal: a material of kind metal, or any material with a hot-working window. A [Hearth](Hearth.md) set beside it keeps the stock hot.

A second tab, **Smith**, does vanilla smithing: a template, a base and an addition in the first three cells, so netherite upgrades and trims still work.

A tool is put together by laying its parts in a crafting grid, not here. Parts for organic stock are made at the [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md); everything else at the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md).
