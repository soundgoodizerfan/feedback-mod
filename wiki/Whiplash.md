# Whiplash

An arm that throws a **barbed hook on a line** from your wrist, so both hands stay free for a sword or a gun. Hold its key to throw; release to pull.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, so it takes the same slot as the [Feedbacker](Feedbacker.md) and the [Knuckleblaster](Knuckleblaster.md): you wear one of the three. It grants its technique itself; no skill needed.

## Getting one

```
P R P
C W C
  L
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate, R a Wrought Iron Rod, C a Chain, W a Grapple Winch (see [Grapple](Grapple.md#crafting)) and L Leather. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md). The recipe appears once you have unlocked the wrought iron plate. The arm comes out wrought iron.

## Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot. Press **B** (*Whiplash*, rebindable) to throw the hook along your look; hold it while the hook flies and, once it bites, while the line holds. Release to pull.

- **A light creature** (lighter than you by body size, the same stand-in as the [parry](Combat.md#parry)'s) is dragged to you.
- **A heavy creature or a block** pulls you to it.
- **A creature** is bitten for **1 damage**.
- **Release before the hook bites** and it is drawn back.
- **Press the key again with a line out** to let go.
- **Jump, or punch with a Knuckleblaster,** to cancel the pull and keep your momentum.

The spear also takes what it touches:

- A **dropped item** is fetched to your feet.
- A **projectile in flight** is held where it hangs, then drawn to you and sent along your look at **1.5 times** its speed, like a parry.
- A **coin** of yours in the spear's path is knocked back up (see Coin flip under [Skills](Skills.md#observation)).

## The line

The line is the arm's strap, wrought iron, so its rating follows wrought iron's yield strength as a [grapple](Grapple.md#the-line-has-a-rating) line's does, and it parts, strains and burns the same way.

## What it costs

Each throw takes **6 stamina**, wears the arm 1 point and has a short cooldown. At its last point the arm stops working.

The left arm is drawn with a green skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.
