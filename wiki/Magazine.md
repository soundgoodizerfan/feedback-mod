# Magazine

Holds up to **10** cartridges of one size for a [gun](Guns.md).

## Crafting

Five Iron Plates.

```
P   P
P P P
```

## Filling it

Put the magazine in a crafting grid with up to ten [cartridges](Ammunition.md) and take it out fuller. All the rounds in one magazine have to be the **same bore**. A magazine that already holds 9 mm rounds won't take 12 mm ones.

The tooltip shows how many rounds it holds and their size.

## Using it

Hold a gun and press **R**. The gun takes the **fullest magazine of its bore** in your inventory, and the old magazine (with whatever was left in it) goes back into your inventory in its place. Reloading takes a moment, and you can't fire until it's done.

A gun holds one magazine plus one round in the chamber, so a full gun shows **11**.

**U** unloads the gun: the magazine and the chambered round both come back to you. Do this if the barrel is *SMOKING* and you don't want a [cook-off](Guns.md#heat).
