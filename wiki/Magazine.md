# Magazine

Holds cartridges of one size for a gun with a **box** or **belt** feed. Guns with a cylinder, tube or barrels hold their rounds themselves and take no magazine (see [Guns](Guns.md#actions-and-feeds)).

## Sizes

The recipe decides the size. All of them are crafted from Wrought Iron Plates, except the belt:

| Magazine | Holds | Recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small Magazine** | 6 | 3 Wrought Iron Plates: `P _ P` over `_ P _` |
| **Magazine** | 10 | 5 Wrought Iron Plates: `P _ P` over `P P P` |
| **Extended Magazine** | 20 | 7 Wrought Iron Plates: `P _ P`, `P _ P`, `P P P` |
| **Drum Magazine** | 50 | 8 Wrought Iron Plates: a ring |
| **Ammo Belt** | 100 | 3 Wrought Iron Nuggets in a row (belt-fed automatics only) |

Any gun of the right bore and feed takes any size.

## Filling it

Put the magazine in a crafting grid with [cartridges](Ammunition.md) and take it out fuller. All the rounds in one magazine have to be the **same bore**. A magazine that already holds 9 mm rounds won't take 12 mm ones.

The tooltip shows how many rounds it holds and their size.

## Using it

Hold a gun and **hold R**. Once R has been held for 1.4 s, the gun takes the **fullest magazine of its bore** in your inventory, and the old magazine (with whatever was left in it) goes back into your inventory in its place. Let go early, fire, or put the gun away and nothing is swapped.

A gun holds one magazine plus one round in the chamber, so a gun with a full 10-round magazine shows **11**. With no magazine in, the HUD says *NO MAGAZINE*.

**U** unloads the gun: the magazine and the chambered round both come back to you. Do this if the barrel is *SMOKING* and you don't want a [cook-off](Guns.md#heat).
