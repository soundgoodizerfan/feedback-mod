# Respirator

A leather mask with a charcoal filter, worn on the head. It cuts the poison from fumes and [fume clouds](Hazards.md#fume-clouds) to a fifth.

## Crafting

```
 Leather   Iron Plate   Leather
 Leather   Charcoal     Leather
           Leather
```

Leather is leather, Iron Plate is an iron plate and Charcoal is charcoal.

## Using it

Wear it in the head slot. Any fume dose, whether from a cloud, a boiling melt, an anode effect or molten leaded solder, gives a fifth of the poison it would. It stacks with the [Fume ward](Skills.md) skill: both cuts apply together.

## How it works

The mask filters. It supplies no air, so it does nothing in a place that has none. It never wears out.
