# Recipe Data Stick

A stick that records one cut from the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md) so a [Power Cutter](Power_Cutter.md) can repeat it.

## Crafting

```
W
C
S
```

W is Copper Fine Wire, C is Ceramic and S is a Steel Plate.

## Using it

A blank stick records when you make a cut at the Stonecutter with the stick in your **off hand**. It keeps that cut and the kind of stock and form the cut was made on. Its tooltip reads "Records a cut to (result) from (stock) stock in the (form) form." A stick holds one cut; making another cut with it in your off hand overwrites it.

Seat the stick in a Power Cutter by right-clicking the machine with it. See [Power Cutter](Power_Cutter.md#how-it-cuts) for what the stick decides.
