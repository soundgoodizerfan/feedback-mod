# Recipe Data Stick

A stick that records one cut from the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md) so a [Power Cutter](Power_Cutter.md) can repeat it, or one assembly from the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) so an [Auto Engineer](Auto_Engineer.md) can.

## Crafting

```
W
C
S
```

W is Copper Fine Wire, C is Ceramic and S is a Steel Plate.

## Using it

A blank stick records when you make a cut at the Stonecutter with the stick in your **off hand**. It keeps that cut and the kind of stock and form the cut was made on. Its tooltip reads "Records a cut to (result) from (stock) stock in the (form) form." The same holds for an assembly taken at the Engineering Table: its tooltip reads "Records an assembly of (result) from (n) kinds of stock." A stick holds one cut or one assembly; making another with it in your off hand overwrites it.

Seat the stick in a Power Cutter or an Auto Engineer by right-clicking the machine with it. See [Power Cutter](Power_Cutter.md#how-it-cuts) and [Auto Engineer](Auto_Engineer.md#recording) for what the stick decides.
