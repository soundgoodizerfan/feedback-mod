# Lead

Lead is a very soft, very dense, low-melting metal. It is the usual bullet metal, the shield against radiation, the plate of the lead-acid battery and a poison. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Lead Ore lies in the Overworld from y -64 to 16, and galena (85% lead) from y -32 to 48, both in the sulfide belt ([Ores](Ores.md)).
- **Melting.** Raw lead and crushed ore melt to molten lead in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md) at a low heat. Galena concentrate roasts to molten lead with oxygen and leaves sulfur, a silver nugget and, one time in ten, a bismuth nugget ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)). Argentite and uraninite leaching leave lead nuggets.

Lead is the softest metal on this list and among the densest. Dense bullets keep their energy, but a lead chamber rates only 0.3 Pu ([Ammunition](Ammunition.md), [Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber)). A lead edge is too soft to mine iron ore. Dense material shields radiation, so lead walls and armor do best ([Radiation](Radiation.md#what-shields)).

Lead is **toxic**: a weapon part made of lead poisons its target for 3 seconds ([Material Perks](Material_Perks.md)), and leaded solder poisons you while you work it ([Soldering Iron](Soldering_Iron.md)).

## What it is used in

- **Lead Plate.** Goes into the [Fission Reactor](Fission_Reactor.md) core, the [Control Rod Assembly](Control_Rod.md), the Radiation Source of the [Dark Cell](Dark_Cell.md), the spent fuel pool of [Nuclear Fuel](Nuclear_Fuel.md#cooling-a-spent-rod), the Evacuated Tube of the [Pneumatic Tube](Pneumatic_Tube.md) and the Flux Meter ([Instruments](Instruments.md#flux-meter)). Lead plates and smooth stone make the reactor's Neutron Reflector.
- **Batteries.** Lead plates are the standard cell: about 85% of the charge returns, and half the capacity is lost at 0 Tu ([Battery](Battery.md)).
- **Neutron absorber.** A bundle of lead in a control rod absorbs neutrons (0.15).
- **Fuse wire.** Blows above about 1.9 A ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Solder.** Leaded solder ([Materials](Materials.md#solders)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
