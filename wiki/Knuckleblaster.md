# Knuckleblaster

A gauntlet for **heavy punches and a shockwave**: one slow blow that hits hard and breaks glass, and a burst that shoves creatures and turns projectiles away from you.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, so it takes the same slot as the [Feedbacker](Feedbacker.md) and the [Whiplash](Whiplash.md): you wear one of the three. It grants its techniques itself; no skill needed.

## Getting one

```
P P P
C E C
  L
```

P is a Steel Plate, C is a Copper Heating Coil, E is an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and L is Leather. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md). The recipe appears once you have unlocked the tempered steel plate. The gauntlet comes out tempered steel.

It is also a **rare find** (about 3%) in dungeon, stronghold and ancient city chests, already 30 to 70% worn.

## Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot and press **G** (the parry key) with **nothing in your hand**.

- **Heavy punch.** A slow blow that deals **6 damage** and knocks the target back hard: the lighter the target, the farther it flies. It also breaks glass and glass panes in reach. It costs stamina and wears the gauntlet.
- **Shockwave.** Click for the punch, then keep the key held for a full second after it and the gauntlet fires a burst ahead of you, in a radius of **3.5 blocks**. Creatures in it take **1 damage** and are shoved away; you are unharmed. Projectiles in it are turned along your look at **1.5×** their speed. The burst costs more stamina and wear.

There is no parry and no healing. The left arm is drawn with a red and black gauntlet skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.

## What it costs

Each punch and each shockwave takes stamina and wears the gauntlet. At its last point it stops working.
