# Hand Tools

The hand tools are the ones you carry and use yourself: a hammer for shaping metal, a wrench for turning blocks, a crowbar for prying fittings off, and the knife, spatula and saw for food and stone. Most are hot-hammered from heated metal in a crafting grid, or have their metal blade shaped at a bench and assembled with a handle. Each has its own page.

- [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md): shapes plates and stock by hand, and is the tool the others are hammered with. Made in a crafting grid from cobblestone and a stick, or a better head shaped at a bench and put on a handle.
- [Hand Knife](Prep_Table.md): cuts food and dough on the Prep Table. Made by shaping its blade hot at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) or [Anvil](Anvil.md), then assembling it with a handle.
- [Sawblade](Sawblade.md): goes in the Stonecutter's saw slot to cut stone and wood. Made by shaping a saw blade hot at the Smithing Table or Anvil, then assembling it.
- [Spatula](Spatula.md): flips food in a [Skillet](Skillet.md). Made from a spatula blade shaped from plate, planks or bone (metal is shaped hot), put with a handle.
- [Wrench](Wrench.md): turns a placed block a quarter turn. Made by hot-hammering four iron ingots in a crafting grid with a hand hammer.
- [Crowbar](Crowbar.md): pries fittings off a machine. Made by hot-hammering two wrought iron plates in a crafting grid with a hand hammer.
- [Tongs](Hazards.md#tongs): hold hot work so it doesn't burn your hand. Made from two rods of one metal at working temperature, a grip and a hand hammer, in a crafting grid.
