# Thorium

A heavy, soft, silvery metal that gives off radiation, found as the ore **monazite**.

## Finding it

Monazite grows in deep veins (Y -48 to 16), with black sand over some of them. The ore and every thorium form are [radioactive](Radiation.md): the ore at 0.1, the metal at 0.25, a quarter of uranium's. A [Survey Meter](Survey_Meter.md) finds the ore; a [Geiger Counter](Geiger_Counter.md) finds it by ear.

## Working it

Monazite melts into thorium (1750 Tu) like any ore, and molten thorium casts into ingots. It makes every metal form.

## What it is for

Thorium stands heat. Baked into tungsten (a thorium nugget with a tungsten ingot at 3200 Tu or more) it makes **thorium-tungsten**, which stands 2800 Tu in service. Carry it with care: see [Radiation](Radiation.md).
