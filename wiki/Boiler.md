# Boiler

Turns heat into **steam**. Sits over a fire like a [Crucible](Crucible.md) does. Its steam drives a [Steam Engine](Steam_Engine.md).

## Crafting

```
P   P
P P P
```

P is a [Steel Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md).

## Using it

1. Put it on top of a [Firebox](Firebox.md), or over lava.
2. **Right-click it with a Water Bucket** to fill it. That's the only way to put water in.
3. Once it's hot enough, it starts turning water into steam.

A boiler needs far less heat than metalwork. It starts boiling well below a "red" glow, so even a modest fire runs it.

## How much steam

Making steam **uses up the boiler's heat**. A weak fire holds the boiler just at boiling and makes a trickle. A hotter fire keeps up with full output. If your engine is starving, the fire is too weak, not the boiler too small. Add air with a [Powered Bellows](Powered_Bellows.md), or a hotter fuel.

At full output, **one boiler keeps one Steam Engine running**.

## Getting steam out

There are no pipes. A [Steam Engine](Steam_Engine.md) placed **touching** the boiler draws steam from it directly. Put a [Valve](Valve.md) between them to cut the steam off.

Remember to refill the water.
