# Reach Probe

A probe that links to a machine and keeps its reach: the hottest it runs, the pressure it holds to, or the speed it turns to. With a linked probe in your inventory, recipe cards in JEI show that machine's reach beside what the recipe asks for.

## Crafting

```
  N
  W
  S
```

N is an Iron Nugget, W is [Copper Wire](Wire_Drawer.md) and S is a Stick.

## Using it

- **Right-click** a machine to link it: a thermal vessel (its temperature ceiling), a [Boiler](Boiler.md) or [Pressure Vessel](Pressure_Vessel.md) (the pressure at its danger line, lower when the shell is worn) or a [Flywheel](Flywheel.md) shaft (its rim speed). The tooltip names the machine and its figures.
- **Sneak and use it in the air** to forget the link.

While you carry a linked probe, a recipe card that asks for a temperature, a reaction pressure or a rabble speed gets an extra row with the linked machine's reach, printed exactly. Under it, **Your reading** says how finely your best carried [instrument](Instruments.md) reads what the recipe asks for. The card gives figures side by side and leaves the decision to you.

Without a probe, the last vessel screen you opened counts as the linked machine for about a minute.
