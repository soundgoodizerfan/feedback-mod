# Drying Rack

A rack that dries food slowly. Right-click with a food to hang one (it holds four); right-click empty-handed to take the last one back. Dried food feeds less but keeps much longer, since spoilage runs with how wet the food is.

- Drying is faster in warmer air, in proportion to its temperature: air at 65 Tu dries about three times as fast as a 20 Tu room. A fire under the rack warms the air, but only up to **65 Tu**, the edge of cooking: it dries, it does not cook. Finer cuts dry faster, heavy foods slower, and a covered dish slower still.
- Rain on an open rack stops the drying completely. Put it under a roof.
- A raw food dried this way is still raw inside. Cook it before you eat it, or accept what is in it.

Recipe: seven sticks.
