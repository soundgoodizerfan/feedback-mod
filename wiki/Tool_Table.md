# Tool Table

Gone. The Tool Table's part making is split across three benches by what the stock is made of, and its tool assembly is done in a crafting grid. A placed Tool Table from before the change no longer exists.

| Stock | Bench | Hand tool | Forged? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metal, or anything with a hot-working window | [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) | [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) | Yes: the forging rhythm |
| Wood, bone, leather, wool, fibre | [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md) | An axe | No |
| Stone, ceramic, glass, crystal | [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md) | None | No |

Every bench shows nothing to make until stock is in the cells, then only the parts that stock can make there: fabric offers bindings and glove bases and nothing else. Guns are made at the [Gun Table](Gun_Table.md) and dishes at the [Prep Table](Prep_Table.md), which work the same way.

See [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md) for what each part is and how a tool is put together.
