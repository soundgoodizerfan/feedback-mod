# Ore Deposits

Vanilla's ores no longer appear when the world is made. In their place are **Feedback's own ores**: Coal, Iron, Copper, Gold, Lapis Lazuli, Redstone, Diamond and Emerald Ore, each in a stone and a deepslate kind, and the Nether's Gold and Quartz Ore. They look the same, dig the same, need the same pickaxe and drop the same things. They are different blocks, and that is the point: every ore in the world is one the mod knows about and places by its own rules.

Vanilla's old ore blocks still exist, so one you find in a structure or take from the creative menu works as before. They just don't grow in the ground any more.

## Finding them

Dig, as before. Each ore sits where it always did.

- **Coal** in the open: high up in the hills, and again as buried seams through the middle of the world. The buried ones show less in cave walls.
- **Iron** in three places: small flecks all through the rock, richer pockets in the middle depths, and plenty high in the mountains.
- **Copper** in the middle depths, in bigger masses inside dripstone caves.
- **Gold** deep, with a rare extra at the very bottom, and in the open on badlands mesas.
- **Lapis** around the low-middle depths, with more buried seams that never show in a cave wall.
- **Redstone** right at the bottom of the world.
- **Diamond** deep, in small flecks, bigger pockets, rare large masses, and buried ones that never show in a cave wall.
- **Emerald** scattered thin through mountain rock, in the mountain and windswept biomes only.
- **Nether gold and nether quartz** in the netherrack, all through the Nether. Gold still angers piglins when you mine it.

## Reading the surface

A vein near enough to the surface sends up a sign: up to three, scattered over its width, standing on dirt, sand or bare stone. Most are a **loose rock**, a small heap of cobble flecked in the colour of the ore beneath. Break one for a few nuggets or a raw piece of what lies below. The deeper the vein, the fewer signs reach the surface; the deep diamond, gold, lapis and redstone veins show only where a mountain stands over them, and the buried ones barely at all.

| Vein | Sign |
| --- | --- |
| Tin, zinc, lead, copper (all copper veins), graphite, galena, pyrite | loose rock of that ore |
| Iron (high, middle and small veins) | wrought iron loose rock |
| Iron gossan | hematite loose rock |
| Magnetite | magnetite loose rock |
| Copper porphyry | chalcopyrite loose rock |
| Zinc blende | sphalerite loose rock |
| Silver lode | argentite loose rock |
| Uranium lode | uraninite loose rock |
| Coal (both seams) | coal loose rock |
| Gold (all three), Lapis (both), Redstone (both), Emerald | loose rock of that ore |
| Diamond (all four) | diamond loose rock; only the shallowest pockets under high ground show |
| Kaolinite | white tulips on the grass above |
| Nether gold and quartz | gold or quartz loose rock on a cavern floor in the netherrack above the vein |

## Crystals and clay

- **Galena** and **Pyrite** are crystal veins in stone, in the middle and lower depths. Both rectify, which is why a cat's whisker detector wants one. Galena also melts down to lead, and a hot enough fire sweats a little silver out of it.
- **Kaolinite** is a soft white clay, in lumps near the surface in the hills. It digs by hand, and fires into firebrick.
- **Magnetite** is a magnetic iron mineral in veins through the low and middle depths, ringed with hematite, with loose rock on the ground above. Four magnetite lumps round a stick (in a diamond) make a compass.

## Mining them

Same tools, same drops. Coal ore gives coal, iron ore raw iron, copper ore several raw copper, gold ore raw gold, lapis ore a handful of lapis, redstone ore a few redstone dust, diamond ore a diamond, emerald ore an emerald, nether gold ore a few gold nuggets and nether quartz ore a quartz. Fortune gives more, and Silk Touch gives the ore block itself. Coal, lapis, diamond, emerald and the Nether's two also drop a little experience, and redstone too. Redstone ore lights up and glows when you touch or mine it, as before.

Iron, copper and lapis want at least a stone pickaxe; gold, redstone, diamond and emerald want an iron one; coal and the Nether's two take any pickaxe.

JEI's **Ore Vein** page lists every vein: its dimension, depth, size, richness and how common it is, and which ores sit in its core, cap, rim and scatter.

## What did not change

Vanilla's two huge veins, the ones that hold raw iron and raw copper blocks, still form in the ground and still hold vanilla's ore blocks. Ancient debris is still vanilla's, and so are the other Nether deposits (gravel, magma, soul sand, blackstone).
