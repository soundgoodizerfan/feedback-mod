# Ore Deposits

Vanilla's ores do not appear when the world is made. In their place are **Feedback's own ores**: Coal, Iron, Copper, Gold, Lapis Lazuli, Redstone, Diamond and Emerald Ore, each in a stone and a deepslate kind, and the Nether's Gold and Quartz Ore. They look the same, dig the same, need the same pickaxe and drop the same things. They are different blocks, and that is the point: every ore in the world is one the mod knows about and places by its own rules.

Vanilla's old ore blocks still exist, so one you find in a structure or take from the creative menu works as before. They do not grow in the ground.

## Finding them

Dig, as before. Each ore sits where it always did.

- **Coal** in the open: high up in the hills, and again as buried seams through the middle of the world. The buried ones show less in cave walls.
- **Iron** in three places: small flecks all through the rock, richer pockets in the middle depths, and plenty high in the mountains.
- **Copper** in the middle depths, in bigger masses inside dripstone caves.
- **Gold** deep, with a rare extra at the very bottom, and in the open on badlands mesas.
- **Lapis** around the low-middle depths, with more buried seams that never show in a cave wall.
- **Redstone** right at the bottom of the world.
- **Diamond** deep, in small flecks, bigger pockets, rare large masses, and buried ones that never show in a cave wall.
- **Emerald** scattered thin through mountain rock, in the mountain and windswept biomes only.
- **Chromite** deep in the lower rock, **pentlandite** (nickel) through the middle depths, **cobaltite** deep, **pyrolusite** (manganese) in the upper rock, **bauxite** (aluminum) high up and near the surface, **wolframite** (tungsten) very deep, **monazite** (thorium) in the lower rock. Monazite is [radioactive](Radiation.md).
- **Spodumene** (lithium) in the upper rock between y 16 and 128, **beryl** (beryllium) between y 32 and 256 in the same mountain and windswept biomes as emerald, **molybdenite** (molybdenum) low, between y -16 and 48, and **dolomite** (magnesium) in broad veins between y 0 and 96.
- **Osmiridium** in river biomes between y 36 and 64 as placer veins, and in an underground sulfide band between y -32 and 40, with loose rock above. It is the source of [heavy core](Heavy_Core.md#making-it).
- **Stibnite** (antimony) in the Overworld between y -8 and 64 and **vanadinite** (vanadium) between y -24 and 40, each in veins with a loose rock above. **Cinnabar** (mercury) in the Nether between y 10 and 117, in the netherrack, with a loose rock above.
- **Cryolite**, rare, in the overworld between y -20 and 60. It melts into the bath for [aluminum](Guide_Making_Aluminum.md).
- **Saltpetre** in cave deposits from Y -16 to 64, with a loose rock on the surface.
- **Deep Dark uraninite** in the deepslate between y -64 and -16, only where the Deep Dark lies; the sculk concentrates the isotope. It is [radioactive](Radiation.md).
- **Nether ores**: **pentlandite**, **wolframite**, **chromite**, **cobaltite**, **uraninite** and **monazite** grow in the netherrack all through the Nether, richer in the hot Nether belt, with the same signs as their Overworld kin. Uraninite and monazite are [radioactive](Radiation.md).
- **Bastion Rubble** in small, rare patches in the Nether between y 30 and 110. Each block breaks into 1 to 3 Worn Netherite Bits, the scrap of [Netherite](Netherite.md#getting-the-scrap).
- **Sulfur** in the Nether, in the netherrack, with a loose rock above.
- **Ilmenite** (titanium) in the netherrack.
- **Nether gold and nether quartz** in the netherrack, all through the Nether. Gold still angers piglins when you mine it.

## Rarity, provinces and companions

**Rarity follows the material.** A common ore (coal, iron, copper) turns up more often than a rare one (diamond, emerald).

**Provinces.** Large areas of the world, a few hundred blocks across, are rich or barren in a group of ores together. Find a belt and more of its ores lie nearby; in a barren area they are few. Each province is drawn separately for each world.

| Province | Ores |
| --- | --- |
| Tin belt | tin, wolframite, molybdenite |
| Porphyry belt | copper, chalcopyrite, gold, silver |
| Sulfide belt | galena, sphalerite, zinc, lead, pyrite, stibnite, cobaltite |
| Pegmatite field | beryl, tourmaline, spodumene, monazite, garnet |
| Mafic complex | chromite, pentlandite, magnetite, osmiridium sulfide |
| Evaporite basin | borax, saltpetre |
| River placers | osmiridium |
| Hot Nether belt | Nether pentlandite, chromite, cobaltite, wolframite, uraninite, monazite |

Ores with no province (coal, iron, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald and others) are spread evenly. The Nether holds a little more ore than the Overworld, and its provinces are milder.

**Companion deposits.** Some veins have partners lying beside them, so finding one tells you what to look for next:

- Galena with sphalerite and a little pyrite.
- Porphyry copper with gold and molybdenite.
- Tin with wolframite.
- Pentlandite with chromite and osmiridium sulfide.
- Beryl with tourmaline and spodumene.
- Gold with silver.
- Lead with zinc.

**Biomes** tilt some ores: gold and saltpetre favor badlands, jade favors mountains. Emerald, beryl, borax, the large copper masses (dripstone caves), the extra gold (badlands) and the river osmiridium stay in their biomes.

## Reading the surface

A vein near enough to the surface sends up a sign: up to three, scattered over its width. Most are a **loose rock**, a small heap of cobble flecked in the color of the ore beneath, standing on dirt, sand or bare stone. Break one for a few nuggets or a raw piece of what lies below (iron loose rock gives cast iron nuggets). Some veins show a second kind of sign beside the rock, and each spot is one or the other: **flowers** that tolerate the metal grow in a loose patch on the grass above (copper flower over the copper veins, zinc violet over the zinc ones, bladder campion over lead and galena), and **stained soil** replaces the dirt in a wider patch (rust-stained soil over iron gossan, black sand over tin, gossan rubble over gold, tarnished soil over the silver lode, sooty soil over the upper coal seam, bleached soil over the uranium lode). Flowers need ground they can stand on; stained soil shows in any biome that has dirt at the surface. Both drop themselves, and stained soil digs like dirt. Stained soil is also a poor ore: put a block through the [ore ladder](Ore_Processing.md#stained-soil-as-ore) for a little metal. The deeper the vein, the fewer signs reach the surface; the deep diamond, gold, lapis and redstone veins show only where a mountain stands over them, and the buried ones barely at all.

| Vein | Sign |
| --- | --- |
| Tin | tin loose rock, or black sand |
| Lead, galena | loose rock of that ore, or bladder campions |
| Graphite, pyrite, saltpetre, sulfur | loose rock of that ore |
| Zinc, copper (both plain veins) | loose rock of that ore, or zinc violets or copper flowers |
| Iron (high, middle and small veins) | iron loose rock |
| Iron gossan | hematite loose rock, or rust-stained soil |
| Magnetite | magnetite loose rock |
| Copper porphyry | chalcopyrite loose rock, or copper flowers |
| Zinc blende | sphalerite loose rock, or zinc violets |
| Silver lode | argentite loose rock, or tarnished soil |
| Uranium lode | uraninite loose rock, or bleached soil |
| Chromite, pentlandite, cobaltite, pyrolusite, bauxite, wolframite, ilmenite, cryolite, spodumene, beryl, molybdenite, dolomite | loose rock of that ore |
| Osmiridium (placer, sulfide) | osmiridium loose rock |
| Monazite | monazite loose rock, or black sand |
| Coal (upper seam) | coal loose rock, or sooty soil |
| Coal (lower seam) | coal loose rock |
| Gold (all three) | gold loose rock, or gossan rubble |
| Lapis (both), Redstone (both), Emerald, Ruby, Jade, Garnet, Tourmaline | loose rock of that ore |
| Diamond (all four) | diamond loose rock; only the shallowest pockets under high ground show |
| Kaolinite | white tulips on the grass above |
| Nether gold and quartz | gold or quartz loose rock on a cavern floor in the netherrack above the vein |

## Gems

Gems grow in veins of their own, each marked by a loose rock. Mined, the ore block drops the cut stone (*Jade*, *Garnet*, *Tourmaline*, *Ruby*, *Beryl*) ([Materials](Materials.md#gems)).

- **Jade** in the Overworld between y 32 and 160.
- **Garnet** in the Overworld between y -32 and 48, the widest of the four veins.
- **Tourmaline** in the Overworld between y 16 and 128, in the narrowest veins.
- **Ruby** in the Nether, between y 10 and 117, in the netherrack.

## Beyond the Overworld

The [Gap](The_Gap.md#salvage) holds asteroids of iron, pentlandite, sperrylite, osmiridium and rare beryl. The crystal world has frequent amethyst geodes ([The Gap](The_Gap.md#the-crystal-world-by-rocket)).

## Crystals and clay

- **Galena** and **Pyrite** are crystal veins in stone, in the middle and lower depths. Both rectify, which is why a cat's whisker detector wants one. Galena also melts down to lead. Galena veins hold a thin scatter of argentite, the silver ore.
- **Kaolinite** is a soft white clay, in lumps near the surface in the hills. It digs by hand, and fires into firebrick.
- **Magnetite** is a magnetic iron mineral in veins through the low and middle depths, ringed with hematite, with loose rock on the ground above. Four magnetite lumps round a stick (in a diamond) make a compass.

## Salt deposits

**Salt Deposits** are blocks lying in disks 2 to 3 blocks in radius in the sand of shallow ocean floors, under water, in ocean biomes only. Dig one by hand or with a shovel for 4 Salt; Silk Touch gives the block itself. Salt is also left by boiling sea water dry in a [Stockpot](Stockpot.md).

## Mining them

Same tools, same drops. Coal ore gives coal, iron ore raw iron, copper ore several raw copper, gold ore raw gold, lapis ore a handful of lapis, redstone ore a few redstone dust, diamond ore a diamond, emerald ore an emerald, nether gold ore a few gold nuggets and nether quartz ore a quartz. Fortune gives more, and Silk Touch gives the ore block itself. Coal, lapis, diamond, emerald and the Nether's two also drop a little experience, and redstone too. Redstone ore lights up and glows when you touch or mine it, as before.

What a mined ore is worth as metal depends on how far you process it; the raw rock gives the least ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md)). Iron ore does not smelt to an ingot ([Guide: Making Wrought Iron](Guide_Making_Wrought_Iron.md)).

Iron, copper and lapis want at least a stone pickaxe; gold, redstone, diamond and emerald want an iron one; coal and the Nether's two take any pickaxe.

JEI's **Ore Vein** page lists every vein: its dimension, depth, size and richness, a rarity word with its share of the dimension's veins, its province, its biome factors, the companion ores it is found with, and which ores sit in its core, cap, rim and scatter.

## What did not change

Vanilla's two huge veins, the ones that hold raw iron and raw copper blocks, still form in the ground and still hold vanilla's ore blocks. Ancient debris is still vanilla's ([Ancient Debris](Vanilla_Changes.md#ancient-debris)), and so are the other Nether deposits (gravel, magma, soul sand, blackstone).
