# Materials

In Feedback, **what something is made of matters more than what item it is**. Machines, magnets and guns don't check for "an iron ingot". They look at the **material**: how hard it is, how dense, whether it's magnetic, how strong. Any item made of that material behaves the same way.

## The metals

| Metal | Hardness | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | very soft | Dense. The classic bullet metal. Useless for guns. |
| Gold | very soft | |
| Tin | very soft | For bronze. |
| Copper | soft | The first metal you'll work. Wire, cable, plates, foil. |
| Zinc | soft | For brass and the [Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md). |
| Brass | medium | Copper + zinc alloy. Good cartridge cases. |
| Bronze | medium | Copper + tin alloy. |
| Iron | medium-hard | **Magnetic.** |
| Cast Iron | hard | **Magnetic.** Can't be rolled, drawn or drilled into parts; good for steelmaking. |
| Steel | very hard | **Magnetic.** Must be worked **red-hot**. See [Making Steel](Steel.md). |
| Hardened Steel | extremely hard | **Magnetic.** Only the [Grinding Wheel](Grinding_Wheel.md) can shape it. |
| Netherite | hardest | **Magnetic.** The strongest gun and engine metal. |

JEI shows each metal's exact hardness on its metalworking pages.

## Forms

Every metal comes in a family of **forms**, each made by a particular machine:

| Form | Made by |
| --- | --- |
| Ingot, Nugget, Block | crafting (nine nuggets ↔ ingot ↔ block) |
| Plate | hammering an ingot |
| Foil | hammering a plate (keep going) |
| Scrap | hammering foil (too far) |
| Rod | [Rolling Mill](Rolling_Mill.md) |
| Wire, Fine Wire | [Wire Drawer](Wire_Drawer.md) |
| Case | drawing a plate |
| Bullet | hammering a nugget, or [casting](Casting.md) |
| Drilled Plate | [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md) |
| Turned Rod | [Lathe](Lathe.md) |
| Ground Ingot | [Grinding Wheel](Grinding_Wheel.md) |
| Swarf | the leftovers of drilling, turning and grinding |

So there's a *Bronze Plate*, *Lead Wire*, *Gold Foil* and so on, for every metal. A few don't exist: cast iron is too brittle to be made into plates, rods or wire, and neither is hardened steel.

## Properties you'll actually notice

- **Hardness** decides what can shape a metal. A blow weaker than the hardness, or a cutting tool softer than the metal, does nothing at all. See [Metalworking](Metalworking.md).
- **Magnetic** metals are pulled by the [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and work as [coilgun](Coilgun.md) slugs.
- **Density** decides how heavy things are: bullets, guns, engines and vehicle plates. Dense bullets keep their energy.
- **Strength** decides how much pressure a gun chamber holds and how fast an engine can rev.

## Where to find them

Copper, iron and gold come from vanilla ores. Brass and bronze are [alloyed](Casting.md#alloys). Steel and cast iron are made in a [crucible](Steel.md).

**Tin, zinc and lead don't generate in the world yet.** For now they only come from the creative menu.
