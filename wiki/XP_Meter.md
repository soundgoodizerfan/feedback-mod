# XP Meter

Reads experience as numbers: the level something stands at, and the points it holds.

Right-click:
- a Crystal Holder, to read the container in it;
- an XP wire or a Creative XP Source, to read the whole run: its level, total points, points moving per tick and points leaking;
- the air, to read the container in your other hand, or yourself if it is empty;
- a player, for theirs;
- a villager, for its trade level and trade XP.

Carried in your inventory, it also turns the words on container tooltips and Jade into figures. It reads levels to whole levels and points to the nearest five; a fitted circuit narrows the step.

Without a meter, containers say only how high their level stands and how full they are. See [Experience](Experience.md).
