# Copper

Copper is the first metal you work: soft, a very good conductor of electricity, and the base of wire, cable, tubing and bronze. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Copper Ore lies in the Overworld from y -16 to 112, with the large masses in dripstone caves. Chalcopyrite, malachite and cuprite carry copper too (chalcopyrite carries iron beside it). See [Ores](Ores.md).
- **Melting.** Put raw copper or crushed ore in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or a [furnace](Furnaces.md) and it melts to molten copper at its melting point. The further the ore is processed, the more metal it gives ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md)).
- **Refining.** Pure copper plates out of brass or scrap in the [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md#the-liquid-picks-the-metal).

Copper comes in the forms listed on [Materials](Materials.md#forms): plate, rod, wire, foil and the rest.

## Figures

| Figure | Copper |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 4 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 8.96 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1085 |
| Toughness | 100 |
| Yield strength (St) | 3.5 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 16.5 |
| Heat conduction | 0.1 |
| Conductivity | 1 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.7 |

Copper is the reference conductor: every other metal's conductivity is read against its 1. Soft and tough, it is easy to work, but a blow weaker than its hardness does nothing ([Metalworking](Metalworking.md)). A copper edge is too soft to mine gold or diamond ore.

## What it is used in

- **Wire and cable.** [Copper Cable](Copper_Cable.md) carries 1, 4, 16 or 64 A by gauge, and every other metal's cable is read against it. Copper wire also goes into the [Arc Furnace](Arc_Furnace.md), the [Breaker](Breaker.md), the [Charger](Charger.md), [Induction](Induction.md) coils.
- **Tubing and buckets.** Two Copper Plates stacked make two Copper Tubing, used by the [Condenser](Condenser.md), the [Fission Reactor](Fission_Reactor.md) and the improved [furnaces](Furnaces.md). Three Copper Plates in a V make a Copper Bucket, which scoops oil and carries melt ([Casting](Casting.md#casting-basin)).
- **Electrodes.** Copper is the cathode of the [Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md) and the noble metal of the [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md). As fuse wire it carries 16 A ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Alloys.** [Bronze](Bronze.md) with tin, brass with zinc, and the other copper alloys on [Casting](Casting.md#alloys).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
