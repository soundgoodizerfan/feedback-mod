# Tungsten

Tungsten is the metal with the highest melting point: dense, very hard and strong, and not tough. It is made by leaching wolframite and precipitating the metal. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Wolframite** (60% tungsten) lies in the Overworld from y -56 to 8 in the tin belt, and in the hot Nether belt from y 10 to 117 ([Ores](Ores.md)). Leach the concentrate with lye in a [Reactor](Reactor.md) to sodium tungstate, then precipitate the tungsten with hydrogen ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).
- **End Stone** is a low-grade ore that takes the same route.

Tungsten is nearly as dense as gold and has the highest melting point of any metal. Its toughness is low. A blow must beat its hardness of 34 before it works at all ([Metalworking](Metalworking.md)). It expands very little when warmed.

## What it is used in

- **Plasma Head.** A Tungsten Plate and Ceramic make the head that cuts conducting metal plate ([Power Cutter](Power_Cutter.md)).
- **Tungsten carbide.** Tungsten carbide powder, about 9 parts carbide to 1 cobalt, is pressed and sintered ([Mixer](Mixer.md), [Furnaces](Furnaces.md#sintering)); its plate makes the Saw Head of the Power Cutter.
- **Neutron absorber.** A bundle of tungsten in a [Control Rod Assembly](Control_Rod.md) absorbs neutrons (0.4).
- **Alloys.** Thorium-tungsten, high-speed steel and stellite ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 4 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
