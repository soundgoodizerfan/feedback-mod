# Machine Head

The replaceable striking head of a [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md) or [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md). It does two jobs: it sets **how hard a blow can land**, and it is **the part that wears**. The machines themselves are neither.

## Making one

Made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) like any modular item:

1. Hammer stock into a **Head Blank** (a [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) in the table). Ingots, blocks, gems or cobblestone all work; the shape picks the size:
   - **Standard**: 4 cells of stock.
   - **Broad-Faced**: 6 cells. Heavier, so a higher ceiling.
   - **Narrow-Faced**: 2 cells. Concentrates the blow: about three quarters of a standard head's ceiling for half the stock.
2. Put the finished blank in the centre of a bench and fit what the head needs to make it.

Any material works, and the material matters. The ceiling comes from the head's **mass**, its **hardness** and its **strength**; a hard but brittle material (diamond, quartz) loses a lot of it, and a soft one (lead) has little. An iron head of four ingots has a ceiling of 24 St.

## Using it

- **Right-click a hammer or press with a head** to seat it. The one it replaces comes back to your hand.
- **Sneak and right-click with an empty hand** to pull it.
- A machine with **no head** does nothing, wears nothing, and says *No head seated*.

## Wear

The head takes the force a machine cannot put into the work (cold or too-hard metal, scrap, gearing past the ceiling). It never breaks and never stops: at worst it bottoms out at half condition and hits softer. A new head resets the machine. The condition stays on the head, so a worn head moved to another machine is still worn. [Calipers](Calipers.md) read the seated head's condition while the machine is stopped.
