# Charging Gantry

A frame on an [electrical](Electricity.md) line. Stand in it wearing an [Exosuit](Exosuit.md) and the suit's [Battery](Battery.md) charges.

## Crafting

```
P W P
P   P
P C P
```

P is a Steel Plate, W is [Copper Wire](Wire_Drawer.md) and C is a [Charger](Charger.md).

## Using it

Connect it to a line like any electrical block. Stand in the frame; only its floor plate is solid. While the line is above the battery's voltage, the battery in the worn suit's chestplate bay takes charge as a placed battery does. Nothing is drawn when you are not wearing a suit or the bay is empty.

## Misuse and hazards

A line over the battery's case band wears the battery, as it does in a placed one. See [Hazards](Hazards.md).
