# Counter

A shop counter that lets a line do business with a villager.

```
P E P
P H P      P oak planks, E emerald, H hopper
```

## Using it

Place it within 8 blocks of a villager. Goods fed in from a hopper or belt are handed to the nearest villager the way a delivery at their screen would be:

- A made piece is judged against the orders the villager has posted today. If it satisfies one, the Counter takes it and pays emeralds by its grade, as at the screen.
- Anything else is offered to what the villager buys, and sold up to the day's limit for each line.

Emeralds come out of the middle slots, and whatever the villager did not take comes out of the last slots. Take them by hopper, belt or hand. Right-click it with an empty hand to see whether a villager is in reach.

## Misuse

The Counter does not choose or read what it is given. Poor work pays little, and a run of similar pieces fills the villager's saturation for days, as at the screen. With nothing sorting upstream, rejected goods fill the return slots and the intake stalls until they are emptied.
