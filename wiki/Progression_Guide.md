# Progression Guide

A loose order of play, from the first fire to oil and circuits. Each stage names what you do and the problem that sends you to the next. Nothing is locked: if you can reach the temperature, strength or hardness, you can skip ahead. The guide only says where the problems usually show up first.

## 1. First fire, clay and copper

You start with stone and wood tools and no fire you control. Build a [Campfire](Campfire.md), then a [Firebox](Firebox.md) or [Hearth](Hearth.md) whose temperature you can read, and fire clay into vessels. A [Crucible](Crucible.md) melts copper, tin and lead, and [Casting](Casting.md) turns the melt into ingots and parts in molds. Bronze and brass are alloys you pour yourself. Shape hot metal with the [Hammer](Hammer.md) at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md), and join parts with solder.

Read: [Heat](Heat.md), [Metalworking](Metalworking.md), [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md), [Hand Tools](Hand_Tools.md), [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md).

**The wall:** copper and bronze are soft, and your fire is too cool to melt iron. Look for a way to push more air through a fire, to hold heat in, and to line a chamber so it survives the temperature.

## 2. Iron

Iron needs a hotter fire that also strips the oxygen from the ore. Feed the fire air with bellows by hand, then with a [Powered Bellows](Powered_Bellows.md). Wrap the chamber in [Insulation](Insulation.md), line it with [Refractory Lining](Refractory_Lining.md) and trim the draft with a [Damper](Damper.md). The [Furnaces](Furnaces.md) page covers the vessels that take the place of the vanilla furnace. Iron ore does not smelt to an ingot: it becomes a spongy bloom that you hammer clean into wrought iron ([Making Wrought Iron](Making_Wrought_Iron.md)), or melts to cast iron, and charcoal steel appears when the iron stays in a narrow temperature window.

Read: [Furnaces](Furnaces.md), [Making Wrought Iron](Making_Wrought_Iron.md), [Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md), [Making Steel](Steel.md), [Fuels](Fuels.md), [Materials](Materials.md).

**The wall:** a tool of fresh steel is either brittle or soft. Look for how heat and cooling change the hardness of a finished part.

## 3. Treated steel and alloys

Quench and temper steel to the hardness a job needs. A hotter [kiln or furnace](Furnaces.md), a [Heat Exchanger](Heat_Exchanger.md) and a [Fluid Burner](Fluid_Burner.md) give finer control of heat. Alloys such as stainless steel and manganese steel, and hard carbides and cermet, are mixed and cast with the [Mixer](Mixer.md). A [Heating Coil](Heating_Coil.md) heats without fuel and smoke.

Read: [Heat Treatment](Heat_Treatment.md), [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md), [Mixer](Mixer.md), [Material Perks](Material_Perks.md).

**The wall:** all of this is hand work, and your own arm is the only source of power. Look for a way to make a machine do the swinging.

## 4. Rotation

Turn a [Hand Crank](Hand_Crank.md) and carry the motion along a [Shaft](Shaft.md) with [Bearings](Bearing.md), [Cogs and Gearbox](Cogs.md) and [Pulleys](Pulleys.md). A [Crank Linkage](Crank_Linkage.md) drives a hammer from a shaft. A [Water Wheel](Water_Wheel.md) runs a mill unattended, a [Flywheel](Flywheel.md) smooths the load, and a [Clutch](Clutch.md) and an [Overspeed Trip](Overspeed_Trip.md) stop it. Where there is no river, a [Boiler](Boiler.md), [Steam Engine](Steam_Engine.md) and [Condenser](Condenser.md) turn fire into a turning shaft. Steam can hurt you, so read [Hazards](Hazards.md).

Read: [Rotation](Rotation.md), [Units](Units.md), [Transmission](Transmission.md).

**The wall:** hand-made parts are loose and rough. Look for machines that cut, press and draw metal to a size.

## 5. The machine shop

Shaft-driven machines shape metal more exactly than any hand. The [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md) and [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md) work hot metal, the [Rolling Mill](Rolling_Mill.md) makes plate, the [Wire Drawer](Wire_Drawer.md) makes fine wire, and the [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md), [Lathe](Lathe.md) and [Grinding Wheel](Grinding_Wheel.md) finish parts with a [Machine Head](Machine_Head.md). Their output is the start of precise gun parts, pipes and cable, and of the [Engine](Engine.md).

Read: [Metalworking](Metalworking.md), [Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md), [Engine](Engine.md).

**The wall:** a shaft carries power only as far as the line of gears. Look for a way to send power through a thin wire.

## 6. Electricity

Make a small charge with a [Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md) or an [Electrostatic Generator](Electrostatic_Generator.md), and make steady power with an [Dynamotor](Dynamotor.md) on a turning shaft. [Copper Cable](Copper_Cable.md) carries it anywhere, and a Dynamotor run as a motor turns it back into rotation at the machine. A [Battery](Battery.md) stores it. The [Heating Coil](Heating_Coil.md), [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md) are the first electric tools. Wrong volts and amps cost you cable, machines and health.

Read: [Electricity](Electricity.md), [Breaker](Breaker.md), [Fuse](Fuse.md), [Hazards](Hazards.md).

**The wall:** a running machine is never told when to stop. Look for a way to make a machine act on what it measures.

## 7. Control

Start with simple stops: a [Timer](Timer.md), a [Bimetallic Strip](Bimetallic_Strip.md), a [Breaker](Breaker.md), a [Fuse](Fuse.md) and a [Relay](Relay.md). [Instruments](Instruments.md) read a machine's real state. A [Controller](Controller.md) runs a program you write with cards, and a [Data Connector](Data_Connector.md) joins it to what it reads and drives. A [Redstone Coherer](Redstone_Coherer.md) and [Cat's Whisker Detector](Cats_Whisker_Detector.md) bring redstone signals into the line. On a [Trace Board](Trace_Board.md), a [Soldering Iron](Soldering_Iron.md) and a [Reflow Oven](Reflow_Oven.md) build a [Basic Electronic Circuit](Basic_Electronic_Circuit.md).

Read: [Programming](Programming.md), [Controller](Controller.md), [Instruments](Instruments.md).

**The wall:** pure metals and fuel fires cannot make everything. Look for substances made from salt, water, air and the oil under the ground.

## 8. Chemistry and oil

Split water and brine in the [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md) into hydrogen, oxygen, chlorine and lye. The [Reactor](Reactor.md) and [Pressure Vessel](Pressure_Vessel.md) make acids, ammonia and [Fertilizer](Fertilizer.md) from what the electrolyser gives. [Oil](Oil.md) comes up from the ground and is split by the [Fractionating Column](Fractionating_Column.md) into [Fuels](Fuels.md) for engines and for the [Thruster](Thruster.md), with a [Flare Stack](Flare_Stack.md) to burn off waste gas and [Gas Tanks](Gas_Tank.md) and a [Gas Compressor](Gas_Compressor.md) to store it. Polymers give insulation and synthetic rubber. [Making Aluminum](Making_Aluminum.md) takes an electrolytic bath.

Read: [Chemicals](Chemicals.md), [Oil](Oil.md), [Pipe](Pipe.md), [Making Aluminum](Making_Aluminum.md).

## Alongside the machines

These paths run in parallel with the ones above.

- **Food and survival:** [Crops](Crops.md) and the [Food](Food.md) chain from foraging to cooking in a [Skillet](Skillet.md) or [Stockpot](Stockpot.md), then preserving with a [Drying Rack](Drying_Rack.md), [Ice Box](Ice_Box.md) and [Cold Room](Cold_Room.md). [Brewing Stand](Brewing_Stand.md) potions need a brew map.
- **Trading:** [Villagers](Villagers.md) take commissions for tools, weapons and armor.
- **Fighting:** [Combat](Combat.md) is timing and stamina first. Assembled gear, then [Guns](Guns.md) built at the [Gun Table](Gun_Table.md), then the [Coilgun](Coilgun.md) and the [Grapple](Grapple.md) widen what you carry.
- **Experience and radiation:** the [Enchanting Table](Enchanting_Table.md) and [Experience](Experience.md) store and spend XP, and [Radiation](Radiation.md) is a hazard you learn to measure and shield against.
