# Chromium

Chromium is a very hard, brittle, corrosion-proof metal. Alone it is little use; alloyed it makes steel stainless. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

Its ore is chromite (70% chromium): Overworld y -48 to 16 in the mafic complex, and the hot Nether belt from y 10 to 117 ([Ores](Ores.md)). A chromite concentrate is smelted with charcoal to molten chromium, leaving iron nuggets ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).

## Figures

| Figure | Chromium |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 20 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 7.19 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1907 |
| Toughness | 3 |
| Yield strength (St) | 15 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 4.9 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0234 |
| Conductivity | 0.13 |
| Corrosion resistance | 1 |

Chromium is harder than steel and does not corrode, but its toughness of 3 is very low, so it is alloyed rather than worked.

## What it is used in

- **Stainless steel.** 16 to 20% of the melt ([Stainless Steel](Stainless_Steel.md)).
- **Alloys.** Nichrome with nickel, stellite with cobalt and tungsten, high-speed steel and redstone-chromium, a drawn alloy of redstone whose fine wire carries data ([Data Connector](Data_Connector.md), [Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Synthetic Pearl.** A Redstone Chromium ingot is one of its inputs ([Synthetic Pearl](Synthetic_Pearl.md)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 3 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
