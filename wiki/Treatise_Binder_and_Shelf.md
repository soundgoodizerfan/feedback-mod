# Treatise Binder and Shelf

The Treatise Binder and the Treatise Shelf carry [treatises](Treatise.md) so that the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) counts them as held. Both take only treatises.

## Treatise Binder

Carries treatises the way a bundle carries items. The treatises inside count as held while the binder is in your inventory.

```
Leather + String + String + Iron Ingot (shapeless)
```

Click a treatise onto the binder to put it in. Click the binder on an empty slot to take one out. Only treatises go in.

## Treatise Shelf

A shelf of 9 slots that opens like a chest. It takes only treatises and binders. Placed touching a Blueprint Table, what it holds counts as held by whoever draws there. Its contents drop when it is broken.

```
P P P
Z Z Z
P P P
```

P is any plank and Z is Paper.
