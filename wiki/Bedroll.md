# Bedroll

A rolled bed for the road. Lie down in it on firm ground at night, or in a storm, and the time skips to dawn wherever you are.

## Crafting

Made at the Tool Table from three parts, as for any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md):

- **Sheet** (Bedroll Sheet): the cloth or leather you lie on, leather or carpet.
- **Fill**: fibrous stuffing between the sheet and the shell. The softer it is, the better the sleep.
- **Shell**: a leather cover.

The [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) shows the **comfort** of a drawing before you make it.

## Using it

Use it on firm ground at night or in a storm. It needs no hostile creature within 8 blocks; otherwise it refuses. A night's sleep:

- skips to dawn,
- gives back a share of your health equal to the bedroll's comfort (softer fill and sheet give more),
- resets the insomnia count that brings phantoms.

A bedroll **never sets your spawn point**. Each use wears it a little.

A bedroll on a pack's [bedroll lashing](Backpack.md#fittings) can be used without taking it out: press the **Bed Down in Pack Bedroll** key (comma unless you set another).

Messages tell you why it will not work: only on firm ground at night or in a storm, or monsters nearby.
