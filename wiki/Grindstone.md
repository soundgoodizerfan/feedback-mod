# Grindstone

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps its old use and adds a new one.

## In vanilla

Right-click it to open a screen. It strips the enchantments off an item (giving back some experience) and combines two damaged items of the same kind into one with more durability.

## In Feedback

Everything above still works. On top of it, the grindstone is where you **regrind the edge** of a [tool or weapon](Tools_and_Weapons.md#edge):

- **Sneak and right-click** the grindstone with an edged tool in hand.
- The edge steps to the next choice: **keen**, **standard**, **blunt**. Keen bites harder and chips sooner; blunt is tougher.
- Each grind costs **2 points of durability**, and never the last one.

The cost is the point. Without it an edge would be a free toggle (keen for the dig, blunt for the walk home), and a choice that costs nothing isn't a choice.

Any block in the `#feedback:grindstones` tag works, so a modpack's grinding block can join. Only things with an edge can be ground: bows, shields, maces and armour have none.
