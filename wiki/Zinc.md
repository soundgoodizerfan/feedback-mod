# Zinc

Zinc is a soft metal with a low melting point that corrodes in place of the metal under it. It is the other half of brass, the anode of the [Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md) and a protective coat. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Zinc Ore and sphalerite (67% zinc) lie in the Overworld from y -32 to 48 in the sulfide belt ([Ores](Ores.md)).
- **Melting.** Raw zinc and crushed ore melt to molten zinc in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md). Sphalerite is roasted and leached with sulfuric acid to Zinc Sulfate Liquor, and the [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md#plated-metal) plates 1 Zinc Nugget per 16 mB; the leach also gives cadmium and indium nuggets ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).

## Figures

| Figure | Zinc |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 3.5 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 7.14 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 420 |
| Toughness | 40 |
| Yield strength (St) | 5 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 30 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0289 |
| Conductivity | 0.29 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.6 |

Zinc expands more than any other metal here and has the lowest corrosion resistance. It is a base metal, far behind copper in the [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md#the-liquid-picks-the-metal): the cell plates copper out of brass and leaves the zinc dissolved.

## What it is used in

- **Brass.** About 2 parts copper to 1 part zinc ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Galvanic Cell.** Zinc is the anode and copper the cathode. A Zinc Ingot restores half a spent anode ([Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md)).
- **Hot dipping.** A finished tool or armor piece dipped in molten zinc, kept at 430 Tu or more, takes a zinc coat; one to three dips make a sound coat ([Casting](Casting.md#hot-dipping)).
- **Fuse wire.** Blows sharply above about 5.3 A, which protects copper cable ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Guns.** A zinc chamber rates 3.3 Pu for a 9 mm bore and 3 mm wall ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).
