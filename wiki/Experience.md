# Experience

Experience is a stored, transferable quantity with two numbers: the **level** it stands at and the **points** held at that level. In your own body the two are locked to the vanilla bar. In a container they are separate: a small container can hold few points at a high level, a big one many points at a low level.

## Containers

An **XP Container** is a vial of crystal, mineral or paper, made at the Stonecutter (crystal, mineral) or the Woodworking Table (paper). Three sizes: a vial, a flask and a carboy, holding one, three and nine times the points of a vial. The **material** sets the top level and how many points a vial holds; the size sets only how many vials' worth. Paper tops out at a low level, quartz and amethyst in the middle, lapis a little higher, sculk highest and roomiest.

Sneak-use a container in the air to move up to 20 points between it and you: the lower level gives to the higher. A [Reservoir](Enchanting_Table.md) attunement on a tool is a container too.

Hold a container and its tooltip says how high its level stands (low, moderate, high) and how full it is (empty, part full, nearly full, full); hold Shift for what the words mean. An [XP Meter](XP_Meter.md) in your inventory turns the words into figures.

## Crystal Holder

A block with one slot. Use a container on it to seat it, an empty hand to take it out. The holder puts the seated container's XP on the network; the XP stays in the container, so taking it out takes the XP with it.

## XP wire

Wire made of a material that conducts XP (paper, quartz, amethyst, lapis, sculk). Holders and wires that touch form one network. Containers on it settle toward one shared level and the total points stay the same. How fast depends on the weakest wire: it moves its conductance in points a tick for every level of difference. A poor wire leaks part of what it carries as XP orbs at the wire; sculk leaks none, paper the most. Two holders touching without wire settle at once.

The Creative XP Source (operator tab) on a network holds the whole run at the level it is set to.

## Where to see it

Jade shows the seated container in words, and whether a run is moving or settled. With an XP Meter carried it shows level, points, flow and leak.
