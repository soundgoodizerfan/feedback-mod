# Gun Stand

The workbench where you design a [gun](Guns.md). You set a handful of options, choose a metal, and print the design onto paper as a **Gun Blueprint**.

There's no crafting recipe for the Gun Stand yet. It's in the creative menu.

## The screen

Use the **< >** buttons to change each option. The spec sheet below updates as you go.

| Option | Choices | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bore** | 5–20 mm | Bigger bullets carry more energy but kick harder. The chamber also holds less pressure at a wider bore. |
| **Barrel** | short / medium / long | Longer barrels give faster bullets and better accuracy, but the gun is heavier and costs more. Gunpowder needs a long barrel to burn completely. |
| **Wall** | thin / standard / thick | How much pressure the chamber can take. Thicker walls are safer, heavier and cost more. |
| **Sight** | iron / reflex / scope | How far aiming zooms, and how fast you can raise it. Better sights cost more. |
| **Launcher** | gun / coilgun | Powder chamber or redstone coil. See [Coilgun](Coilgun.md). |
| **Material** | set by the sample slot | What the gun is made of. |

**Cost** is shown at the bottom: how many ingots of the chosen metal, plus Blocks of Redstone for a coilgun.

## Slots

- **Sample slot**: put in any piece of the metal you want, such as an ingot, plate, rod or nugget. That sets the material. **The sample isn't used up.** New designs start as steel.
- **Paper slot**: Paper to print on. You can also put a Gun Blueprint here to load its design, change it, and print over it.

Press **Print** to turn the paper into a blueprint.

## Choosing a metal

The stand only accepts metals that are strong enough to make a gun from and come as ingots. Stronger metals hold far more pressure:

- **Netherite** is the strongest by a wide margin.
- **Steel** and **hardened steel** are the practical choices.
- **Iron**, **cast iron**, **bronze** and **brass** make guns, but weaker ones. A thick wall helps them a lot.
- **Copper**, **zinc** and **gold** guns are damaged by standard ammo at a standard wall.
- **Tin** and **lead** are so soft that the gun is wrecked almost at once. Lead is for bullets.

Heavier metals make heavier guns, which heat up more slowly.

Watch the **Chamber** line on the spec sheet. If it's red, the gun can't take a standard round safely. See [Overpressure](Guns.md#overpressure).

## Building the gun

Take the blueprint to a crafting table with exactly the quoted number of ingots (and redstone blocks for a coilgun), in any arrangement. You get the gun and the blueprint comes back.
