# Calipers

A measuring tool. It turns "kind of flattened" into an exact number, and it tells you exactly how worn a machine is.

## Crafting

```
P   P
  N
```

P is a [Copper Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md) and N is an Iron Nugget.

## Measuring work in progress

Without calipers, a half-worked item tells you what it **needs** as a number (*"Needs 14 Fu"*) but only roughly how far it's **got**: *Barely marked*, *Taking shape*, *Visibly worked*, *Nearly there*.

**Carry calipers anywhere in your inventory** and that changes to an exact figure, like *"Calipers: 9 / 14 Fu"*. You see it:

- on the item's tooltip,
- in Jade, on whatever is sitting in a hammer, press, mill, drawer, drill, lathe or grinder.

You don't have to hold or click anything. Having them on you is enough.

You don't need calipers to make a plate; the words are enough to avoid ruining one. You need them to **aim** at something that only lasts a blow or two, like copper foil, and hit it every time.

## Measuring machine wear

**Right-click a machine** with the calipers to measure its condition exactly, e.g. *"Calipers: 85% condition"*.

**The machine has to be stopped.** On a running machine you'll get *"Stop the machine before measuring it."* Getting the figure costs you a stopped line, so decide whether it's worth it.

Without calipers, Jade still warns you when a machine is worn (*Head is marked / battered / spent*), just not by how much.
