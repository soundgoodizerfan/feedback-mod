# Ages

Six ages name how far your materials have come: Stone, Bronze, Iron, Steel, Machine and Alloy. Each is an advancement you earn by making something. No age is required for any recipe, tool or machine.

## Entering an age

You enter a material age by making your first tool or part of that age's materials, in the crafting grid, at a part bench or at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md). Gear you find or trade for never counts.

| Age | Materials | Entered by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stone | wood, bone, stone, granite, diorite, andesite, basalt, deepslate, tuff, obsidian | your first tool or part of any of them |
| Bronze | copper, tin, bronze, brass, lead, zinc, gold, silver | your first tool or part of any of them |
| Iron | wrought iron, cast iron, iron | your first tool or part of any of them |
| Steel | steel, tempered steel, hardened steel, manganese steel | your first tool or part of any of them |
| Machine | none | the first part cut on a [Lathe](Lathe.md), [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md) or [Grinding Wheel](Grinding_Wheel.md); players standing nearby get it too |
| Alloy | stainless steel, nickel, chromium, aluminum, titanium, tungsten, cobalt, the carbides, cermet, netherite, osmium and their alloys | your first tool or part of any of them |

Ages follow in this order in the advancement tree, but nothing stops you making an Iron tool before a Bronze one.

## Sciences

Each science's first study hangs on the age that provides its tools. Once the age is entered, the sciences under it can be taken in any order.

| Age | Sciences |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | the heat chain: brass, and air through iron |
| Iron | electricity, heat and blaze, XP and sculk |
| Steel | chemistry, ender |
| Machine | radiation, flight |
| Alloy | fission |

## Treatises

Each [Treatise](Treatise.md) volume names the age its specimen belongs to. Reading is never gated by age.

See the [Progression Guide](Progression_Guide.md) for the order of play.
