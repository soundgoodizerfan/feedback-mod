# Rolling Mill

A pair of driven rollers that rolls ingots into rods.

## Crafting

```
I B I
S S S
```

I is an Iron Ingot, B is a [Bearing](Bearing.md) and S is a [Shaft](Shaft.md).

## Using it

The mill sits **directly on a shaft line**; connect it from any side. The faster it turns, the faster it rolls.

- **Right-click with an item** to put an ingot in (or swap it for what's there).
- **Right-click with an empty hand** to take it out.
- Hoppers can take items out too.

## What it makes

**Ingot → Rod**, for every metal.

There's nothing further to roll a rod into. That makes the mill more forgiving than the [hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md): a finished rod doesn't get ruined if you leave it. But the mill keeps rolling, and **rolling a finished rod wears the mill**. Take the rod out promptly, or use a hopper.

The mill rolls hard enough for steel (heat steel to working temperature first; see [Metalworking](Metalworking.md#hot-metal)).

## Why not just use a hammer?

The hammer needs a linkage and someone to stop it in time. A mill running into a hopper can be left alone. It costs more to build and to drive, but it needs less attention.

Rods go on to the [Wire Drawer](Wire_Drawer.md) and the [Lathe](Lathe.md).
