# Shock Capsule

A block of iron packed with graphite, made to be set off by a blast. A blast of the right size squeezes the graphite into Diamond Grit, the first step to a synthetic [Diamond](Materials.md#synthetic-diamond).

## Crafting

```
P P P
P G P
P P P
```

P is an Iron Plate ([Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md)) and G is [Graphite](Materials.md#graphite).

## Using it

Place it, step back and set off a blast beside it. The capsule is used up and its product pops out where it stood. The size of the blast decides what comes out:

| Blast radius | Examples | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| under 2.5 | | the capsule is left whole, to try again |
| 2.5 to 5.5 | creeper, TNT, respawn anchor | 4 **Diamond Grit** |
| over 5.5 | charged creeper, end crystal | 1 Graphite |

A [Demolition Charge](Demolition_Charge.md) of your own design works the same way: only its blast radius counts. Too weak a blast does no harm to the capsule; too strong a one vaporizes the carbon, and it comes back as plain graphite.

Eight Diamond Grit press into a Diamond Seed ([Materials](Materials.md#synthetic-diamond)).
