# Shulker

The shulker's shell is a light, tough ceramic. Feedback uses it in [Elytra](Elytra.md) and keeps shulker boxes out of the [Ender Port](Ender_Port.md) system.

## What vanilla does

A shulker drops a shell, which crafts into a shulker box.

## What Feedback does

- The shell is a **ceramic**: light, tough and heat-resistant, so it is a material like any other.
- A shulker box or Shulker Shell beside an Ender Port blocks pairing, and a shulker box never crosses a port.
- Five shells and three phantom membranes craft an elytra.
