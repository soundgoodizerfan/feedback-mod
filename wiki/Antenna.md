# Antenna

A whip of metal cut to a length, with an optional coil of wire wound on its foot. Fitted to a [Radio Set](Radio_Set.md), it replaces the set's antenna length and metal.

## Making it

Cut the **Antenna Whip** at a part bench ([Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md)) from rod or wire stock of a metal that conducts electricity, to a length from 100 to 1500 mm. Lay the whip in the crafting grid to get the Antenna. No blueprint is needed.

A **loading coil** is wire (or fine wire) of a conducting metal put in with the whip. It adds length, so a short whip can hear a low band. A wire that conducts worse than copper adds less.

The tooltip shows the whip's length, its conductivity and the loading.

## Using it

- **Use it with a radio set in the other hand** to fit it. The set takes the antenna's length (whip plus loading) and its metal, and the antenna is used up.
- **Right-click it onto a radio set in an inventory** does the same.

The set's tooltip then shows the fitted antenna length. A broken antenna cannot be fitted.

## How it works

A set hears its band best when its antenna is a quarter of that band's wavelength; see [Tuning](Radio_Set.md#tuning) for the length each band wants. A short whip suits a high band and a long one a low band. The metal matters as well: a metal that conducts worse than copper hears less at any length, and one that conducts better does not hear more than a tuned copper whip.
