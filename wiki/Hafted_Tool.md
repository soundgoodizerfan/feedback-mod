# Hafted Tool

Every tool you hold by a haft is one item, the **hafted tool**: a pick, an axe, a shovel, a hoe, a sword, a spear, a maul, a sickle, a cleaver, a hand hammer, a knife, a file, a screwdriver. It is a **haft** with one to three **faces** on it, and what it does is what its faces do. Nothing in the game lists a "pickaxe" or an "axe" as a thing of its own; the name you see is worked out from the faces you put on.

## Faces and the haft

A face is a head, a blade or a point made at a bench like any part ([Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md#making-the-parts)): a pickaxe head, an axe head, a shovel head, a hoe head, a maul head, a sickle head, a cleaver head, a sword blade, a pike head, a hammer head, a knife blade, a file head or a screwdriver bit. The haft is one to four sticks, rods or bones (a sword's grip is leather or a bound grip). Lay them in the 3 by 3 grid of the [Crafting Table](Crafting_Table.md) or the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md), one item to a slot, any positions:

- **One face** and a haft make an ordinary tool. A short haft with a long blade is a sword; a face on a long haft is a spear or a pick.
- **Two or three faces** make a tool nobody wrote down. The first face in reading order (top row first, left to right) is the **front**, the one the swing lands with; the next is the **back**, the third the **top**.
- A **hammer head with no haft** is the head a machine takes ([Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md), [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md)); it is the only face that is a tool on its own.

Everything else is optional and added afterwards on the Fit tab of the Engineering Table: a **binding** where head meets haft (it makes the joint stronger), a **guard**, a **pommel**, a **tip**, a **spring**, a **lanyard**, an **edge guard**. Each sits on a mount the item offers (see [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md#fit)).

## What it does

The tool does what its faces do, together:

| Face | Digs | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pickaxe head | stone and ores | |
| Axe head | wood | strips logs, scrapes copper, scrapes off wax |
| Shovel head | dirt, sand, gravel | flattens paths |
| Hoe head | crops and soil | tills |
| Maul head | wood, and stone at half speed | a heavy head smashes on a fall |
| Sickle head | plants | reaps its neighbours too |
| Sword blade | cobweb | sweeps |
| Pike head | | thrusts and charges, or is thrown |
| Hammer head | | lands the hand's blow: a grid craft, a machine's head |
| Knife blade, file head, screwdriver bit | | the knife, the file and the driver the benches ask for |

A pick face and an axe face on one haft dig both, and an axe face with a hoe face strips and tills. What a head's shape adds (a prospector's pick, a billhook, a bearded axe) rides with that face.

Each face **wears on its own**. Mining wears the face that dug the block, a blow wears the front face. The durability bar shows the most worn face. A worn-out face stops working but the others still do; the tool is spent only when every face is.

## Names

A hafted tool is named after what it does: a hatchet, a felling axe, a heavy pick, a claymore, a javelin, a scythe, a sledgehammer by the face and its size. A **mattock** is a pick with a hoe face, a **pulaski** an axe with a hoe face, a **hammer axe** an axe with a hammer face; a **halberd** an axe with a spear head, a **lucerne hammer** a hammer with a spear head, a **bec de corbin** a pick with a spear head, a **pick hammer** a pick with a hammer; other pairs are named by the two faces ("Pick-Axe"). You can rename a tool on a blueprint.

## Treatises

The faces of a maul, a cleaver and a spear need Weapons Treatise I ([Treatise](Treatise.md)). A tool with one of them is assembled at the Engineering Table, which checks the volume; the plain crafting table refuses it.

## Wearing it out

A worn face can be filed back at a bench ([Filing](Smithing_Table.md#filing)). A tool whose faces are all spent is broken, not gone: it is repaired on the Repair tab of the Engineering Table.
