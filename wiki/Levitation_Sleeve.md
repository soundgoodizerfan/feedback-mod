# Levitation Sleeve

A sleeve of diamagnetic material that cuts the drag of a [Shaft](Shaft.md), [Flywheel](Flywheel.md) or [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md) while a powered [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) touches it.

## Crafting

One plate and one rod of the same diamagnetic material, anywhere in the grid, make one Levitation Sleeve in that material. See [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals) for the diamagnetic materials.

## Using it

Use it on a shaft, flywheel or rotary bearing to fit it. Each holds one sleeve; breaking the block drops the sleeve. Jade shows *Cushioned by a field* while a powered magnet touches the block and *Dry* otherwise.

## How it works

While a powered Electromagnet touches the fitted block, its drag falls by up to 90 percent. A free rotary bearing's damping falls the same way. The cut is smaller for a weaker field and for a material of lower diamagnetism.
