# Spoon

A modular hand tool for working a [Stockpot](Stockpot.md): hold it to stir, tap it to skim.

## Crafting

Shape a **Spoon Bowl** (from a plate, planks or bone; metal is shaped hot at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) or [Anvil](Anvil.md)) and put it with a handle (stick or bone) at the Tool Table, as for any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md).

## Using it

Aim at a stockpot with the spoon in hand.

- **Hold use to stir.** The stir runs at full pace for as long as you hold. It keeps the bottom from scorching and mixes the pot.
- **Tap use to skim.** A press shorter than about a third of a second lifts scum off the top. A skim straight after another does nothing.

Skim before you stir: stirring while scum is on top folds it into the pot for good. [Stockpot](Stockpot.md#stirring-and-skimming) has what stirring and scum do to the food.

A bigger bowl stirs harder and skims more per stroke.

## Wear and hazards

Stirring wears the spoon one point every 5 seconds; each skim that lifts scum wears it one point. A broken spoon does nothing.

A pot hotter than the handle's material stands burns your hand once a second while you stir. See [Hazards](Hazards.md#hot-things).
