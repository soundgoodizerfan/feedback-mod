# Metals

Fifteen metals have a page of their own: how to get each, what it is good for and what it goes into. Sorted by the age each belongs to, then by how a player meets them. The table of every metal and its figures is [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals); the [ages](Ages.md) say where your materials have come to.

| Metal | Age | One line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Copper](Copper.md) | Bronze | The first metal you work: soft, the reference conductor, wire and tubing. |
| [Tin](Tin.md) | Bronze | Soft and low-melting; hardens copper into bronze and makes solder. |
| [Bronze](Bronze.md) | Bronze | Copper with tin: shafts, bearings and machine plates. |
| [Lead](Lead.md) | Bronze | Soft and dense; bullets, radiation shielding, batteries. Toxic. |
| [Zinc](Zinc.md) | Bronze | Makes brass, the Galvanic Cell anode and a protective coat. |
| [Gold](Gold.md) | Bronze | Soft, dense, never corrodes; takes enchantment best. |
| [Silver](Silver.md) | Bronze | The best conductor; its smite hurts the undead. |
| [Iron](Iron.md) | Iron | Pure, magnetic iron; ore does not smelt to it directly. |
| [Steel](Steel.md) | Steel | Iron with carbon: boilers, engines, plates; quench-hardens. |
| [Stainless Steel](Stainless_Steel.md) | Alloy | Steel with chromium and nickel; does not corrode. |
| [Nickel](Nickel.md) | Alloy | Magnetic and tough; battery cathode and alloy partner. |
| [Chromium](Chromium.md) | Alloy | Very hard and brittle; makes steel stainless. |
| [Aluminum](Aluminum.md) | Alloy | Light and soft; made by electrolysis, never smelted. |
| [Titanium](Titanium.md) | Alloy | Light, strong and corrosion-proof; stores hydrogen. |
| [Tungsten](Tungsten.md) | Alloy | The highest melting point; plasma heads and carbide. |

Wrought iron, cast iron, brass, the other alloys and every metal not listed here are on [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals). Alloy mixtures are on [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md#alloys), ores on [Ores](Ores.md) and their processing on [Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md).

Each page prints the same figures from JEI's *Material Stats* page. **Hardness** and **yield strength** are in St and decide what shapes the metal; **density** is Mu per mB; **melting point** is in Tu; **expansion** is the millionths of its length it gains per Tu; **conductivity** is read against copper's 1; **corrosion resistance** runs from 0 to 1 and higher is better. See [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals) for what each figure does.
