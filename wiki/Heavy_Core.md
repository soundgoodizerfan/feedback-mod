# Heavy Core

The densest stock in the game: osmium's own form. Osmium is dense (**22.6**, about three times iron), very hard and brittle, and melts only at 3033 Tu, so its heavy core is pressed from osmium powder and sintered, never cast. Trial chambers hold the very same block, and either one works anywhere a Heavy Core is wanted.

## Obtaining

Take one from a trial chamber, or make it.

## Making it

1. **Find osmiridium.** The ore forms **placer veins** in river biomes (Y 36 to 64) and a **sulfide band** underground (Y -32 to 40). A loose rock marks the vein. It drops Raw Osmiridium.
2. **Leach it** in a [Reactor](Reactor.md#the-reactions) with sulfuric acid and oxygen. The product is **osmium tetroxide**, a toxic gas. Keep room in the output tank: gas with nowhere to go forms a [fume cloud](Hazards.md#fume-clouds).
3. **Reduce it** with hydrogen in a Reactor. The product is an **Osmium Sponge**.
4. **Mix** 7 sponge to 1 nickel in the [Mixer](Mixer.md#mixing-powders-for-pressing) for **Osmium Powder**.
5. **Press** the powder in the [Mechanical Press](Mechanical_Press.md#pressing-powder) into an **Osmium Green Compact**.
6. **Sinter** three compacts in the [Arc Hearth](Arc_Furnace.md#sintering-under-gas) under **argon** or **nitrogen**, between **1800 and 2150 Tu**, best at 1950. The result is a Heavy Core, the same block a trial chamber holds.

The sinter fails two ways. Above **2300 Tu** the grains coarsen into a weak **Porous Sinter**. With no protective gas the powder oxidizes to osmium tetroxide instead: the batch is lost and a fume cloud stays where the hearth stands. Argon comes from [air separation](Chemicals.md#air-separation); nitrogen also comes from a catalyst bed.

## Using it

A Heavy Core is not hammered, drawn or machined. Its use is whole, as osmium's **heavy core** form: the head of a **mace** or a **maul** (a head and two handles, put together in a crafting grid; see [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md)).

A mace hits with its fall: the damage grows with how far you drop, **and with how heavy the head is**. A heavy core is the full mace. A block of iron on a mace works, but it's much lighter and hits much softer. The head is also a lot to hang on a handle: put it on an ordinary stick and the joint works loose at once. The mace's own shaft, a **Breeze Rod**, is strong enough to carry it. A mace found in a chest or given in creative is converted into a modular mace with a Breeze Rod handle and a polypropylene string binding; vanilla's mace recipe does not exist.
