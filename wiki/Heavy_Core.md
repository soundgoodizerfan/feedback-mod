# Heavy Core

The dense block found only in trial chambers, and the one material that exists to be heavy. It's **about three times as dense as iron**, very hard and brittle, and it **can't be melted** in any vessel in the game. So it is found, never made, and it can't be hammered, drawn or machined.

Its only use is whole, as the head of a **mace** (`M / S / S`, see [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md)).

A mace hits with its fall: the damage grows with how far you drop, **and with how heavy the head is**. A heavy core is the full mace. A block of iron on a mace works, but it's much lighter and hits much softer. The head is also a lot to hang on a handle: put it on an ordinary stick and the joint works loose at once. The mace's own shaft, a **Breeze Rod**, is strong enough to carry it.
