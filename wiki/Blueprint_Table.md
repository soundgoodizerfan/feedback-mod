# Blueprint Table

A table for drawing a tool, weapon, shield, bow or piece of armour on paper and **reading its numbers before you make it**. Guns are drawn at the [Gun Stand](Gun_Stand.md) instead.

## Crafting

```
P P
T T
```

P is Paper and T is a Crafting Table.

## Using it

Right-click it to open the drawing. Pick, with the arrows and toggles:

- a **type** (pickaxe, chestplate, bow...),
- one of its **forms** where it has several: which parts it has and how long its handle is,
- the **optional parts** (a tip, a binding) as toggles,
- a **material for each part**: drop a sample item of that material in the part's slot,
- a **shape** for each part that has them: the standard one or a variant (a rapier blade, a hatchet head), and for a twin-headed tool's two heads which kind of head each is. Hover a shape to read what it does,
- the **edge**, on a tool that has one.

The table offers exactly what the [Tool Table](Tool_Table.md) can make, because it asks the same rules: if a drawing is on the sheet, those parts put on the table make that tool. Heat and forging flaws are not part of a drawing: a blueprint is paper, and flaws are what forging adds.

The screen shows the design's figures, **exactly**, and any traits and adjectives it earns. Requirements and plans are free; what a finished piece *has* still costs an instrument ([Balance Scale](Balance_Scale.md), [Scratch Files](Scratch_Files.md)).

**Print** turns a sheet of paper into a **Blueprint** carrying the design and its figures. Put a Blueprint back on the table to open it for drawing again. The table keeps nothing; the paper comes back to you when you close it.

## What a blueprint is for

Mostly it's a plan to look at. The one thing that **needs** one is the [crossbow](Tools_and_Weapons.md#crossbow-blueprint), the only tool with a mechanism whose parts must fit each other: put any Crossbow Blueprint in the plan slot of the [Tool Table](Tool_Table.md) with the parts. The table keeps it. The parts don't have to match the drawing. Repairs and swaps never need one.
