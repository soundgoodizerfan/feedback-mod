# Tuned Eye

A pearl tuned to a bonded [Ender Port](Ender_Port.md) pair. It tells you where the other end of the pair is.

## Crafting

An Ender Pearl or [Synthetic Pearl](Synthetic_Pearl.md) + Redstone dust, shapeless.

## Using it

- **Use it on a port** to tune it to that port's pair: *"The eye tunes to this pair."*
- **Use it in the air** to read the distance, the compass side and the height of the other end, even when it is far away or in an unloaded chunk: *"The other end: 120 blocks north, 8 up."*

In the air, a needle around the crosshair points the nearest of the four compass ways to the other end, with the distance and height under the crosshair.

If the eye is not tuned it says to use it on a port first. If the other end is not placed anywhere it says so, and if the other end is in another dimension it says that.
