# Crafting Table

A vanilla block. It still crafts everything it did; Feedback changes what it makes of tools and armor.

## What vanilla does

A 3×3 grid for shaped and shapeless recipes, including every vanilla tool, weapon and piece of armor from fixed materials (wooden, stone, iron, golden, diamond).

## What Feedback does

- **Vanilla's tool, weapon and armor recipes do not exist.** Tools are [assembled from parts](Tools_and_Weapons.md#putting-one-together) of any fitting material at a bench, and [armor](Armor.md) from a plate; the crafting grid does not assemble them. The only tools it still makes are the first wooden and stone pickaxe, axe and shovel, laid out as vanilla lays them with one leather added beside the handle as the binding, and a leather glove from four leather.
- **It makes hand work.** A Hand Hammer and one workpiece is a single blow: see [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md).
- **Anything that burns is a torch head.** Put it over a stick (one above the other) for four torches: charcoal, coal, wool and the like. Gunpowder and buckets of lava do not count.
