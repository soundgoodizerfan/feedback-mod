# Crafting Table

A vanilla block. It still crafts everything it did; Feedback changes what it makes of tools and armor, and lets it put simple modular items together from parts.

## What vanilla does

A 3×3 grid for shaped and shapeless recipes, including every vanilla tool, weapon and piece of armor from fixed materials (wooden, stone, iron, golden, diamond).

## What Feedback does

- **It swaps a part.** Lay one finished simple modular item in the grid with replacement parts or stock; the part of that role is replaced, the old part comes back in the grid and the item wears one point. See [Repair tab](Engineering_Table.md#repair-tab).
- **Vanilla's tool, weapon and armor recipes do not exist.** Tools are [assembled from parts](Tools_and_Weapons.md#putting-one-together) of any fitting material, and [armor](Armor.md) is shaped from a plate. The only tools the grid still makes from raw items are the first wooden and stone pickaxe, axe and shovel, laid out as vanilla lays them with one leather added beside the handle as the binding, and a leather glove from four leather.
- **It assembles simple modular items from parts.** Lay the parts in the grid in any positions, one item to a slot, and the result appears: a pickaxe from a head, two handles and a binding, a sword from a blade, a guard and a grip. The parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md), [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md) or [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md). Items drawn with a [blueprint](Blueprint_Table.md) (guns, engines, boilers and the rest) and the types that need a [treatise](Treatise.md) (spear, maul, cleaver, demolition charge) are assembled at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) instead, which also asks for [fasteners](Fasteners.md), plays a hand-work game and judges the fit.
- **It makes hand work.** A Hammer and one workpiece is a single blow: see [Hammer](Hammer.md).
- **Anything that burns is a torch head.** Put it over a stick (one above the other) for four torches: charcoal, coal, wool and the like. Gunpowder and buckets of lava do not count.
- **Machines are crafted at the Engineering Table.** Most machines (a Lathe, a Mixer, a Steam Engine) ask for a hand tool and fasteners. The recipe pages say which.
