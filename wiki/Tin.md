# Tin

Tin is a soft, low-melting metal that corrodes slowly. Its main job is to harden copper into [bronze](Bronze.md); it also makes solder paste, fuse wire and a protective coat. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Tin Ore lies in the Overworld from y 0 to 64 in the tin belt. Black Sand carries a little tin too ([Ores](Ores.md), [Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#stained-soil-as-ore)).
- **Melting.** Raw tin or crushed ore melts to molten tin in a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md) at a low heat. A tin concentrate is reduced with charcoal ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).

| Heat conduction | 0.0167 |
| Conductivity | 0.15 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.9 |

Tin melts at a low heat and is very soft: a tin edge is too soft to mine iron ore.

## What it is used in

- **Bronze.** About 9 parts copper to 1 part tin ([Bronze](Bronze.md)). Pewter, babbitt, bell metal and galinstan also take tin ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Solder.** A Tin Ingot pastes a [Trace Board](Trace_Board.md) or a [Basic Electronic Circuit](Basic_Electronic_Circuit.md); solders are on [Materials](Materials.md#solders).
- **Fuse wire.** Tin blows near 2.8 A, which protects standard copper cable ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Hot dipping.** A finished tool or armor piece dipped in molten tin, kept at 240 Tu or more, takes a tin coat that adds its corrosion resistance ([Casting](Casting.md#hot-dipping)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
