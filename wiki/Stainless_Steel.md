# Stainless Steel

Stainless steel is steel with chromium and nickel: strong, hard and almost immune to corrosion, which is why it lines reactors and holds nuclear fuel. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Making it

Melt [steel](Steel.md), [chromium](Chromium.md) and [nickel](Nickel.md) into the same [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md) and hold the mixture inside the window:

| Metal | Share of the melt |
| --- | --- |
| Steel | 70 to 78% |
| Chromium | 16 to 20% |
| Nickel | 6 to 10% |

About 74% steel, 18% chromium and 8% nickel sits in the middle. Outside the window the tank looks empty and nothing pours; add whichever metal is short. JEI's *Mixing* card shows the window. See [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md#alloys).

## Figures

| Figure | Stainless steel |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 20 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 7.9 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1400 |
| Toughness | 100 |
| Yield strength (St) | 14 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 17 |
| Heat conduction | 0.00399 |
| Conductivity | 0.025 |
| Corrosion resistance | 1 |

Stainless steel is harder, tougher and stronger than steel, and resists corrosion completely. It conducts heat and current poorly.

## What it is used in

- **Fission plant.** Stainless Steel Plates go into the [Fission Reactor](Fission_Reactor.md) core, its fuel channels and coolant parts, and into every kind of fuel rod ([Nuclear Fuel](Nuclear_Fuel.md)).
- **Instruments.** A Stainless Steel Plate goes into the Flux Meter ([Instruments](Instruments.md#flux-meter)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 5, the best ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
