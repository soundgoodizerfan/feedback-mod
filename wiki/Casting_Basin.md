# Casting Basin

A trough that takes the pour from a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or vessel set above it and holds the melt while it sets. It holds up to four molds, or serves as an open pool when it holds none.

## Crafting

```
  B B B
  B   B
```

B is a Brick.

## Using it

- **Place a crucible or vessel directly above it.** Its melt runs down into the basin.
- **Right-click** the basin to open its four mold slots. Only molds fit.
- **Sneak and right-click with an empty hand** to knock out whatever has set. Anything still molten stays.
- **Right-click with a Water Bucket** to quench: everything in the basin drops to room temperature at once.
- **Right-click with a full Copper Bucket** of melt to pour the bucket into the basin, as a vessel above would.

## How it works

With molds set, the melt fills them in order, first slot to last: each mold takes its [full 144 mB](Casting.md#molds), then the next. Each is an ordinary filled mold, as hot as the pour, cooling by itself; you can lift it out and use it by hand instead of knocking it out. Mold slots that hold a different melt are skipped.

With no molds, the basin is an **open pool** that takes one block's worth, **1296 mB**, and cools as a single heap. Once it has set, knocking it out gives the block of that metal, or nine ingots where the metal has no block.

The basin keeps pouring into whatever has room and does not know whether the last pour has set. Leave a pour in and the next one lands on top of it.

See [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md) for melts, molds and set temperatures.
