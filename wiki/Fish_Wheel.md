# Fish Wheel

A [Water Wheel](Water_Wheel.md) with baskets on its blades. It turns in a current as any wheel does, gives half the power, and catches fish while it turns.

Craft it with a Water Wheel and a [Creel](Creel.md), shapeless. Place and drive it like a Water Wheel. It draws a catch from the same fish the water gives a rod, never treasure, and more blades in the current catch faster. An empty hand empties the baskets, and a hopper can take from them. A full basket stops the catch, not the wheel.
