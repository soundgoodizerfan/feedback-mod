# Car Design Stand

The bench where a vehicle's drivetrain is designed. It is two copper plates over two crafting tables. The same block is the **Engine Stand**; Car Design Stand is the name it carries in the game.

Open it and pick a tab:

| Tab | Designs | Prints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Engine** | Cylinders, bore, stroke, compression and the metal. See [Engine](Engine.md). | An Engine Blueprint. |
| **Transmission** | The number of gears (2 to 6) and each gear's ratio. See [Transmission](Transmission.md). | A Transmission Blueprint. |

Both work the same way: put a sample of a metal in the **sample slot** (it isn't used up), put **Paper** in the paper slot and press **Print**. To edit an old design, put its blueprint back in the paper slot, change it and print again. The blueprint is then crafted with the metal's ingots into the finished block.
