# Insulation

Packed terracotta and wool. Build it against a [Crucible](Crucible.md) and the crucible loses less heat to the room.

## Crafting

```
T W
W T
```

T is Terracotta and W is any Wool. Makes **two**.

## Using it

Place insulation blocks touching the crucible's sides. Each one cuts the heat the crucible loses to the room by **20%** (multiplying: four blocks leave about 41%), up to **four**. Jade shows *"Insulated on N sides."*

## What it does

- The crucible **settles closer** to its fire's temperature.
- It **holds its heat longer** when the fire goes out or the air is cut.

What it **can't** do is make the crucible hotter than its fire. If you need more heat, you need a hotter fire ([Powered Bellows](Powered_Bellows.md)).

## Is it always better?

No. Insulation makes a crucible slower to **cool**. If your process overshoots, an insulated crucible takes longer to come back down. Holding a steady band for a long time (steel) loves insulation. A job that needs the temperature to *drop* on cue (tempering) may want [Dampers](Damper.md) instead, or as well.

Insulation only works on crucibles; the [furnaces](Furnaces.md) are built as one sealed wall already.
