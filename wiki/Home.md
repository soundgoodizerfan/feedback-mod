# Feedback Wiki

**Feedback** is a Minecraft tech mod built around one idea: **machines don't know when the job is done.** A hammer keeps hammering after your plate is finished, a crucible keeps heating after your steel is ready, and whatever is being worked on keeps changing. Plates turn into foil, steel burns. Measuring, timing and controlling your machines so they stop at the right moment is the game.

New here? Start with [Metalworking](Metalworking.md), then [Heat](Heat.md).

## A few things worth knowing

- **Instruments work from your inventory.** Carry [Calipers](Calipers.md), a [Thermometer](Thermometer.md), an [Electroscope](Electroscope.md) or a [Galvanometer](Galvanometer.md) and Jade and tooltips show exact figures instead of words.
- **What you need is always shown exactly; what you have costs an instrument.** JEI and tooltips give requirements as hard numbers. The state of your machine is words until you measure it.
- **Machines only wear when misused.** A correctly built line never wears out. If something is worn, you did something specific to it.
- **Nothing is locked.** There are no tiers. If you can reach the temperature, strength or hardness, you can do it, even if it's unreliable at first.
