# Stonecutter

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps what it does and gives it a second job.

## In vanilla

It cuts one stone block into stairs, slabs, walls and the like: one input, pick the result.

## In Feedback

That stays, as the second tab, **Cut**: the same recipes and results, one input consumed per result. The first tab, **Parts**, shapes parts from every other stock (stone, ceramic, glass, crystal). Nothing here is forged, and a [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) goes in the hammer slot.

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); organic ones at the [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md).
