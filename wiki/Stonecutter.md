# Stonecutter

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps what it does and makes it a part-making bench.

## In vanilla

It cuts one stone block into stairs, slabs, walls and the like: one input, pick the result.

## In Feedback

The stonecutter is a [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md) with a **copper circular sawblade**. Crafted with a copper plate over a Woodworking Table. It needs **no hand tool**.

That vanilla cut stays, as the second tab, **Cut**: the same recipes and results, one input consumed per result. The first tab, **Parts**, shapes parts from stone, ceramic, glass and crystal stock. Nothing here is forged. Put stock in the cells and the list shows only what that stock can make here.

So the benches scale with the blade: complex wooden tools need only an axe, stone ones need the copper blade, and metal ones a smithing table and a hammer.

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); organic ones at the [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md). The screen is cobblestone.
