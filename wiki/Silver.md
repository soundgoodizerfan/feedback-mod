# Silver

Silver is a soft, bright metal that conducts electricity better than any other here. Weapons made of it hurt the undead. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

Silver comes from argentite (80% silver), which fills its own silver lode in the Overworld from y -48 to 32 in the porphyry belt, and shows up in a thin scatter through galena veins. Tarnished Soil carries a little silver ([Ores](Ores.md), [Ore Deposits](Ore_Deposits.md)). Roast the argentite concentrate, leach it with brine and hypochlorite in a [Reactor](Reactor.md) to Silver Chloride, then precipitate the silver with hydrogen. Galena smelting also leaves a silver nugget, and leaching gold leaves two ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).

Silver is a better conductor of both current and heat than copper, and it is soft. It carries the **smite** perk: a weapon part made of silver deals +2.5 damage against the undead ([Material Perks](Material_Perks.md#silver)). Silver holds two enchantment effects, one fewer than gold ([Enchanting Table](Enchanting_Table.md#how-strong-and-how-many)).

## What it is used in

- **Filaments.** A Silver Ingot with four Calcined Blend makes a Sheathed Billet, which is rolled and drawn to filament wire ([Materials](Materials.md#superconductor)).
- **Silvered rounds.** Smite on a bullet's metal hurts the undead ([Ammunition](Ammunition.md)).
- **Alloys.** Electrum with gold ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 1 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
