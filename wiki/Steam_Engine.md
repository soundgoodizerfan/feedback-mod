# Steam Engine

Turns steam into [rotation](Rotation.md).

## Crafting

```
P P
S S
```

P is a [Steel Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md) and S is a [Shaft](Shaft.md).

## Using it

Place it **touching a [Boiler](Boiler.md)** (or anything else that offers steam, including other mods' steam). It pulls steam in by itself. A shaft connects at either end of its axle.

When it has enough steam it runs, and when the steam runs out it stops and the line coasts down. It's heavy, like a flywheel, so it smooths out the speed of the line.

## How much it can drive

About as much as **most of a [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md)**. One engine on its own can't quite carry a hammer. Put two engines on the same line, each with its own boiler, or share the line with a [Water Wheel](Water_Wheel.md).

## Controlling it

Put a [Valve](Valve.md) between the boiler and the engine to shut the steam off. The engine stops when its own small store runs dry. A [Controller](Controller.md) can work the valve.

Steam engines run at a modest speed. They never trip an [Overspeed Trip](Overspeed_Trip.md) unless you gear them up.
