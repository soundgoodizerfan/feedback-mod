# Thrown and Imbued Potions

## Throwing

Sneak and use a bottle of potion to throw it. Every potion can also be drunk, and the fire and explosion potions then act on the drinker (see [Brewing Stand](Brewing_Stand.md)). The thrown bottle breaks on the first thing it hits and splashes its potion on every living body within about two and a half blocks, you and mobs included. Bodies at the center get the full length, and it falls off to nothing at the edge; the level is the bottle's own after its potency has faded. A dud draught cannot be thrown.

The **fire** potion is a quick heat release: creatures in reach are scalded by heat (worse per level, softer with distance, answered by fire resistance) and set alight, and flammable blocks next to the burst catch fire. The **explosion** potion detonates where the bottle lands, like TNT, breaking blocks and hurting bodies; the higher the level, the bigger the blast.

### Thrown effects that differ from drinking

Where a potion is not listed here, thrown it gives its drunk effect to every body in reach. The effects of [Brewing Stand](Brewing_Stand.md#potion-effects) list what each does when drunk.

| Potion | Thrown |
|---|---|
| Green thumb | One large growth pulse where the bottle lands, then five lighter sprinkle passes over the following moments. No hunger cost. |
| Quench | Hot machines and hot dropped items within reach cool quickly, fire blocks go out, and burning bodies within five blocks are doused. |
| Static ground | Leaves a zone of held ground for the length of the potion: anyone standing in it takes only the ward fraction of electric shock damage. |
| Antivenom | The effect, and any poison on a body in reach is cured. Works on mobs. |
| Radiation ward, thornskin, haste | Applied to every body in reach, mobs included. |
| Adrenaline | Bodies in reach, mobs too, cannot heal for the length. |
| Second wind | Refills 30% of the stamina bar per level for players in reach, less with distance. No payback stall afterward. Mobs are untouched. |
| Slipstream | Mobs and other non-players get the slowness at once, with no speed burst first. |
| Springheel | Every body in reach is launched upward once, and the next landing lets only 25% of the fall damage through. |
| Steady hand, fine palate, assay | Players only. |

Fine palate or assay thrown over dropped laced food reveals it to the thrower on the action bar: fine palate says the food tastes laced, assay names the potion. See [Brewing Stand](Brewing_Stand.md#lacing-food).

## Imbuing

A tool can be soaked in a potion so that its blows carry the potion's effect for a while. Hold the tool in one hand and the potion in the other and sneak and use: the bottle is spent and the tool takes a **soak**. Only sword-type and axe-type weapons (swords, axes, spears, mauls, twin blades, cleavers and sickles) take one, and only potions of an elemental effect do it: poison, fire and frost.

Each blow you land lays the effect on the creature you hit and spends one of the soak's hits: twelve for fire and frost, twenty-four for poison, which clings to the blade longer (fewer if the potion had faded). A soak is not worn down by waiting. The effect it lays is weaker and shorter than the potion itself: half the level, a fifth of the length, at most eight seconds. The tool's tooltip shows the soak and the hits left. Soaking again replaces the old soak. An enchantment is unaffected.

## Fired from a gun

A brewed potion in a [Reloading Press](Reloading_Press.md)'s payload slot is sealed in a glass vial in the round (a *potion round*, see [Ammunition](Ammunition.md#payloads)). The vial breaks on the first thing the round meets and lets the potion go the way a thrown bottle does, over half the reach and once.
