# Tilt Hammer

A wooden helve rocking on posts over a cobblestone anvil, with a cast iron hammer head fixed to its end. It is the [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md) for the start of the iron age: it comes with a head, and it is crafted at the plain crafting table, with no [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) recipe, bronze or treatise needed.

## Crafting

```
L L L
F I F
B B B
```

L is any log, F is any wooden fence post, I is an Iron Ingot (cast iron) and B is Cobblestone. Mine it with an axe.

## Using it

It is driven, set up and read exactly like the Mechanical Hammer: put it directly in front of a [Crank Linkage](Crank_Linkage.md), put a workpiece on the anvil with right-click, and see [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md) for what the blows make, the cam, forging at a bench, gearing and Jade readings.

The difference is the head. A cast iron head is seated when the block is placed. No other hammer can be put in, and the head cannot be pulled out. Its blow ceiling is that of the cast iron hammer it carries, and it wears like any seated head ([Wear](Mechanical_Hammer.md#wear)). An [Arc Welder](Arc_Welder.md) mends it. When you need a harder blow than the cast iron head can land, build a Mechanical Hammer and seat a better head.
