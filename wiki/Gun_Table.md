# Gun Table

The bench where a [gun](Guns.md) is made. It takes **only guns**, and it has a slot for a **round**. A gun is a modular item like a pick or a sword: parts of a material, each in a shape, and everything the gun does comes from them.

Crafted from two Wrought Iron Plates over two Crafting Tables.

## What it does

It has a hammer slot (every act costs the hand hammer a point) and works as every bench does: what is in the centre decides what the fan shows (shapes for stock, fittings for a part, parts for a gun).


- **Stock in the centre.** Shape a part from stock. The fan shows every gun part and its shapes: a **receiver** as a handgun, rifle, shotgun or machine gun; a **barrel** in a calibre class; a **slide** or **bolt** as an action; a **sight** as a reflex or a scope; and the fittings.
- **A part in the centre.** Fit parts to it, up to sixteen, with a **Plan** slot for the gun's **blueprint** (print one at the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md)) and a **Round** slot. The parts imply the gun; you never choose a type.
- **A gun in the centre.** Its parts are the fan: swap a part, add a fitting, a sight, a second barrel, a coil winding or a grip wrap, or take it apart.

Machined parts (from the [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md), [Lathe](Lathe.md) and so on) go in the slots just the same: see [Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md).

## The spec sheet

Put a **round** in the Round slot and hold Shift over a gun in the centre to read the gun's spec sheet against that round: chamber charge against rating (red if over), speed, energy, report, kick, spread and cycle. The table changes nothing; it shows the interaction so you can see which side to change. If the round does not fit the bore it says so.
