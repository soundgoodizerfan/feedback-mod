# Gun Table

The bench where a [gun](Guns.md) is made. It is the [Tool Table](Tool_Table.md) with one difference: it takes **only guns**, and it has a slot for a **round**. A gun is a modular item like a pick or a sword: parts of a material, each in a shape, and everything the gun does comes from them.

Crafted like the Tool Table's cousin: two Iron Plates over two Crafting Tables.

## What it does

It has the Tool Table's three tabs and its hammer slot (every act costs the hand hammer a point):

- **Parts.** Shape a part from stock. The list shows every gun part and its shapes: a **receiver** as a handgun, rifle, shotgun or machine gun; a **barrel** in a calibre class; a **slide** or **bolt** as an action; a **sight** as a reflex or a scope; and the fittings.
- **Assemble.** Sixteen part slots, a **Plan** slot for the gun's **blueprint** (print one at the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md)) and a **Round** slot. The parts imply the gun; you never choose a type. The left column names the gun and what each slot filled.
- **Rework.** A finished gun in the tool slot: swap a part, add a fitting, a sight, a second barrel, a coil winding or a grip wrap, or take it apart.

Machined parts (from the [Drill Press](Drill_Press.md), [Lathe](Lathe.md) and so on) go in the slots just the same: see [Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md).

## The spec sheet

Put a **round** in the Round slot and the left column adds the gun's spec sheet against that round: chamber charge against rating (red if over), speed, energy, report, kick, spread and cycle. The table changes nothing; it shows the interaction so you can see which side to change. If the round does not fit the bore it says so.
