# Flare Stack

Burns off gas nobody wants. It is the safe vent for a line that makes more than you can use: a gas that is burned stays out of the air, where a leak is a [hazard](Hazards.md).

## Crafting

```
P   P
P P P
```

P is a [Steel Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md).

## Using it

Run a [Pipe](Pipe.md) to it (or a [Gas Tank](Gas_Tank.md)'s outlet). It holds **4000 mB** and burns **20 mB a tick** of whatever it holds, steadily, whatever the fluid is. While it burns it is **lit**: it gives off light and flame, and a lit flare **counts as an open flame**: a cloud of fuel vapor that drifts onto it goes off.

A line that makes gas faster than 20 mB a tick fills the flare and then backs up along the line; it does not burn faster. Put a [Gas Tank](Gas_Tank.md) ahead of it to ride out surges, or fit more than one.

A flare burns without making power. If the gas is worth burning for work, send it to a [Gas Turbine](Gas_Turbine.md) or a [Fluid Burner](Fluid_Burner.md) first.
