# Locked Joint

A micro-block joint that holds two bodies together as one rigid piece. It splits a build into parts that can be put back separately, or anchors a build to a block in the world.

## Crafting

```
IBI
```

I is a Wrought Iron Plate and B is a [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md).

## Using it

It works only as a [micro-block](Micro_Core.md). Placed as a full-size block it says *"It only works as a micro-block: put it in a micro cell."*

1. Put it in a micro cell with its front toward the part to split off.
2. **Use it with an empty hand** to assemble what is joined to its front face into a second body, held rigidly to the first.
3. **Use it again** to put the body back, at the nearest of the 24 ways a cube can be turned.

Placed against a world block, it anchors the build to that block.
