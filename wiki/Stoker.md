# Stoker

A screw feed that pushes solid fuel into the [Firebox](Firebox.md) in front of it, driven by a vertical shaft.

```
P X P
P H P      P wrought iron plate, X shaft, H hopper
```

A hopper or belt fills it from any side but the front. Three drum sizes, stepped by right-clicking with an empty hand, set how much each turn moves. A full firebox refuses the fuel and the feed stalls; the stoker costs only its own power while it waits. It starts and stops from a [Controller](Controller.md) or redstone.
