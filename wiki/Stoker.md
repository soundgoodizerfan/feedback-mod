# Stoker

A screw feed that pushes solid fuel into the [Firebox](Firebox.md) in front of it, driven by a vertical shaft.

```
P X P
P H P
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate, X is a [Shaft](Shaft.md) and H is a Hopper. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 2 [fasteners](Fasteners.md).

A hopper or belt fills it from any side but the front. Three drum sizes, stepped by right-clicking with an empty hand (a strip under the crosshair lists the sizes with the current one lit), set how much each turn moves. A full firebox refuses the fuel and the feed stalls; the stoker costs only its own power while it waits. It starts and stops from a [Controller](Controller.md) or redstone.
