# Puddling Furnace

A furnace with a stirring rod, turned by a shaft into its back. It turns an Iron Ingot (cast iron) into a clean **Iron Bloom** with little slag in it. See [Guide: Making Wrought Iron](Guide_Making_Wrought_Iron.md).

## Crafting

```
B B B
S F R
B P B
```

B is any brick, S is a [Shaft](Shaft.md), F is a [Furnace](Furnaces.md), R is a Wrought Iron Rod and P is a Wrought Iron Plate. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [hammer](Hammer.md) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md), rivets or machine bolts. It needs Materials Treatise II: Metallurgy and Heat Treatment ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

It is a furnace in every way except the stirring: nine slots, a fuel slot, a heat gauge, a sealed body. Put in an **Iron Ingot** and an oxidizer: **hematite, magnetite, limonite** (lump, crushed, concentrate or dust) or **Burnt Iron**. Run a [Shaft](Shaft.md) into the **back** of the furnace, opposite the door. It adds a load of **60 Su** to the shaft.

- Hold it between **1250 and 1450 Tu**, fastest near 1350.
- Keep the shaft between **20 and 60 RPM**. Slower or faster and the charge crusts: progress stops.
- Past **1500 Tu** the oxidizer eats the iron and leaves Burnt Iron.

The result is an Iron Bloom with a little slag (*a little slag in the iron*). Shingle it at an [Anvil](Anvil.md#shingling) or put it under a [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md).

### The rabble window

Right of the heat gauge, a window shows the stirring rod end-on, turning at the speed of the shaft. It is still when the shaft is stopped. Under it a speed dial marks the working speed band of the recipe being run, with a needle at the rod's speed. The needle is plain inside the band and the warning color outside it, so a charge that is not changing because the rod is stalled or racing shows why on the screen.
