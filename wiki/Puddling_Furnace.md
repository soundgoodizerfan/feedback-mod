# Puddling Furnace

A furnace with a stirring rod, turned by a shaft into its back. It turns cast iron into a clean **Iron Bloom** with little slag in it. See [Making Wrought Iron](Making_Wrought_Iron.md).

## Crafting

```
B B B
R F I
B B B
```

B is a Brick, R is a Wrought Iron Rod, F is a [Furnace](Furnaces.md) and I is a Wrought Iron Plate.

## Using it

It is a furnace in every way except the stirring: nine slots, a fuel slot, a heat gauge, a sealed body. Put in **cast iron** and an oxidizer: **hematite, magnetite, limonite** (lump, crushed, concentrate or dust) or **Burnt Iron**. Run a [Shaft](Shaft.md) into the **back** of the furnace, opposite the door. It adds a load of **60 Su** to the shaft.

- Hold it between **1250 and 1450 Tu**, fastest near 1350.
- Keep the shaft between **20 and 60 RPM**. Slower or faster and the charge crusts: progress stops.
- Past **1500 Tu** the oxidizer eats the iron and leaves Burnt Iron.

The result is an Iron Bloom with a little slag (*a little slag in the iron*). Shingle it at an [Anvil](Anvil.md#shingling) or put it under a [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md).
