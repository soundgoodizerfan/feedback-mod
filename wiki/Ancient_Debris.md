# Ancient Debris

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps the block and its place in the Nether, and changes everything done with it.

## What vanilla does

Ancient debris is smelted or blasted into netherite scrap. Four scrap and four gold ingots make a netherite ingot. A netherite ingot, a template and diamond gear in a smithing table make netherite gear.

## What Feedback does

Ancient debris is mined as before (a pick of hardness 40 digs it), but it is not smelted: furnaces and blast furnaces do not turn it into scrap, and the scrap-and-gold recipe does not exist. The [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) does not upgrade gear to netherite either.

Debris and scrap are metal to melt. At 2600 Tu they become Molten Cast Netherite, which is cast and soul-quenched into a netherite ingot. See [Netherite](Netherite.md).
