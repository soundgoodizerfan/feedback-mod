# Pressure Gauge

Turns "the boiler is groaning" into a number.

## Crafting

```
G
T
P
```

G is a Glass Pane, T is a Copper Tubing and P is a [Copper Plate](Mechanical_Hammer.md).

## Using it

**Right-click** a Boiler, a Pressure Vessel, a [Pipe](Pipe.md) or a [Gas Tank](Gas_Tank.md) to read its pressure in Pu on the action bar. It only reads; it can't vent or relieve anything.

## It's rough

The gauge reads in steps of **0.5 Pu**. The danger line is 5 Pu, critical is 7 and a boiler starts to seep at 3.5, so you can see the danger line coming without reading the exact figure.

## Fitting a circuit

Put the Pressure Gauge and a live [Basic Electronic Circuit](Basic_Electronic_Circuit.md) in a crafting grid, in any arrangement: the Pressure Gauge comes out carrying the circuit and reads in finer steps (the usual 0.5 Pu). A quiet circuit takes the step down to about half; a noisy one, such as a cold joint, helps little; a dead one cannot be fitted. A fitted Pressure Gauge still never reads the exact figure. Its tooltip says how fine it reads, and its signature on readings says it is circuit-fitted.
