# Ores

Every ore that bears a metal, the metal and share it carries, and where its veins lie. Sorted alphabetically by ore. Gems, coal, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald are on [Ore Deposits](Ore_Deposits.md); the metal an ore gives depends on how far you process it ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md)). Ilmenite, dolomite and beryl never melt to metal; they are [reduced](Ore_Processing.md#reducing-refractory-ores).

The share is the part of the ore that is that metal. Depths are y levels. A province is a belt of the world where a group of ores lies together ([provinces](Ore_Deposits.md#rarity-provinces-and-companions)).
