# Vice

A bench block that clamps a piece while you work it with a hand tool. Your other hand stays free, and a clamped piece takes each stroke better than one held in the hand, less the play in the jaw screw.

## Crafting

```
P   D
R R R
B B B
```

P and B are Wrought Iron Plates, D is a Wrought Iron Drilled Plate and R is a Wrought Iron Rod. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 machine bolts ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)). It needs Mechanical Treatise I: Engines, Flywheels and Dynamotors ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

1. **Right-click with a thread rod**, rolled or cut ([Lathe](Lathe.md#what-it-makes), [Rolling Mill](Rolling_Mill.md)), to seat it as the jaw screw. The screw it replaces comes back. With no screw the jaws do not close.
2. **Right-click with a piece** to close the jaws on it.
3. Look at the vice and **hold right-click with a hand tool** (a [hammer, file or drill](Hand_Tools.md)). The tool works the clamped piece where it sits, one stroke about every third of a second, as when you [work a piece in your hands](Hand_Tools.md#filing-a-rod). Your off hand is free.
4. **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the piece out, then the screw.

Working stops when you let go, when the jaws open or empty, or when you walk away.

## How the screw matters

A piece held in the hand shifts and loses part of every stroke. A clamped piece loses only the play in the jaw screw. A **cut** thread has little play, a **rolled** thread more, and a softer metal more than a harder one.
