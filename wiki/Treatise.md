# Treatise

A treatise is a numbered book on one **school** of engineering. Holding a volume lets the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) draw blueprints of the types that volume covers and lets the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) assemble them. It is kept, never used up.

## Volumes

Each school has one item per volume. Every volume is a subject of its own: holding Weapons Treatise II does not give you Weapons Treatise I, and nothing in one school opens a type in another. A volume opens **types** (drawn at the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) and assembled at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md)) and **machines and parts** (crafted at the Engineering Table).

## Obtaining

- **Write one.** Craft a **Blank Treatise**, put it in the blank slot on the Treatise tab of the Engineering Table, put an item of that volume's types, or a machine that names it first, in the specimen slot and press **Take apart**, which opens the learning game: calipers measure each part of the specimen (hold to open the jaws, let go when they just touch the part; too tight crushes the reading, too loose is a guess). The size the jaws have to find is the part's own: its largest measured dimension, or the size of its stock if it has none, with your own reading error on top of it and the readings are inked onto a notebook page. How well you read it is the quality of the **notes** the treatise keeps (guessed, rough, fair, clean, exact); you always get the volume. The item is taken apart and the Blank Treatise becomes that volume. See [Specimens](#specimens) for where to get one.
- **Research one.** A Blank Treatise can be written at the [Research Table](Research_Table.md) for 40 experience points with the volume before it held, and the [study prerequisites](#study-prerequisites) held, by playing rows of concept cards for a target score on the notebook page; other volumes you hold boost the score (see [Treatise research](Research_Table.md#treatise-research)).
- **Find one.** A treatise of a random school and volume turns up, rarely, in dungeon, mineshaft, desert temple, stronghold and bastion chests.
- **Sight a planet.** Aerospace Treatise IV comes with your first resolved [telescope sighting](Telescope.md#sighting-a-planet), together with a [Sky Notebook](Telescope.md#sky-notebook).
- **Buy one.** Weaponsmiths, toolsmiths, cartographers, librarians, clerics and masons sell volume I of their school at a high trade level, and the later volumes at higher levels; clerics sell Chemical I and II, and weaponsmiths all three Weapons volumes. See [Villagers](Villagers.md#blueprints).

### Blank Treatise

```
Paper + Paper + Leather + Ink Sac (shapeless)
```

### Specimens

A specimen is one finished item of a type. It is something to take apart on the Engineering Table's [Treatise tab](Engineering_Table.md#treatise-tab) with a Blank Treatise. Specimens turn up in structures, come from other players, or are built once you can build the type.

## Using it

A treatise counts as held, at the Blueprint Table and at the Engineering Table, when it is:

- loose in your inventory,
- inside a [Treatise Binder](#treatise-binder-and-shelf) in your inventory, or
- inside a [Treatise Shelf](#treatise-binder-and-shelf) touching the table, or
- inside a [Treatise Binder](#treatise-binder-and-shelf) in the Engineering Table's binder slot.

Without a volume, the types it covers are left out of the Blueprint Table's type row and list altogether, so nothing of them can be picked or drawn, and the Engineering Table does not offer to assemble them: its status line names the volume it needs. A machine that needs several volumes is crafted only with all of them held; the status line names the first one missing. The treatise's tooltip lists every type it opens and names the [age](Ages.md) its specimen belongs to ("Its specimen is the work of the Iron Age"); reading is never gated by age. The same holding rule decides which part shapes the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) fan (and the Anvil, Woodworking Table, Stonecutter and Prep Table) lists, with a binder in your inventory or a shelf touching the table counting: parts of a gated type stay out of the fan until its volume is held. Treatises stack to sixteen. Copying a blueprint needs no treatise, and neither do repairing an item and taking it apart.

## Treatise Binder and Shelf

The Treatise Binder and the Treatise Shelf carry treatises so that the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) and the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) count them as held. Both take only treatises.

### Treatise Binder

Carries up to sixteen treatises the way a bundle carries items. The treatises inside count as held while the binder is in your inventory, or in the binder slot of the Engineering Table.

```
S L S
  N
```

S is String, L is Leather and N is an Iron Nugget.

Click a treatise onto the binder to put it in. Click the binder on an empty slot to take one out. Only treatises go in.

### Treatise Shelf

A shelf of 9 slots that opens like a chest. It takes only treatises and binders. Placed touching a Blueprint Table or an Engineering Table, what it holds counts as held by whoever draws or assembles there. Its contents drop when it is broken.

```
P P P
Z Z Z
P P P
```

P is any plank and Z is Paper.
