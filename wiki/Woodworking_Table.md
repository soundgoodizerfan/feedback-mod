# Woodworking Table

A block with no vanilla original.

## Crafting

A carpet (any wool carpet) on top of a Crafting Table.

## What it does

An **axe** in its own slot instead of a hammer (any axe, vanilla or modular; each act costs it a point). No hand saw or hand axe exists.

The screen is the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md)'s: a center slot, a **fan** of nodes around whatever is in it, and a column of slots on the right. Putting a part or a tool in the center works as described there. Stock in the center works like this:

- **Stock** in the center: the fan shows every shape that stock can become, as icons. Shapes the pile is too small for are faint. Hover one for a ghost of the part by the result slot and a few bars (hardness, toughness, weight); click it to make it. A part takes only the stock it needs and the rest comes back as smaller items: a block that makes a one-ingot part gives the part and eight more ingots (ingots, for stone). **Break down** turns a big piece (a block) into ingot-sized pieces without making anything.

What the stock offers here:

- **Shapes:** parts from organic stock: wood, nether wood, bamboo, bone, leather, wool and fiber. Tool heads, wooden hammer heads and knife blades, armor pieces, shield faces and guards all come from it. Nothing here is forged: a shape comes out as soon as you click it. The fan shows only what that stock can make: fabric offers bindings and glove bases and nothing else. Every wooden part can be made from one plank; a log or a plank counts as one ingot of stock.
- **Saw:** a second folder tab above the panel. Put planks or a log in the center; the cuts appear in the fan, and the shapes stay on the **Parts** tab. It makes what a crafting grid makes from planks and sticks alone: stairs, slabs, doors, trapdoors, fences, signs, boats, chests, bowls, sticks and the like (a stick counts as half a plank). **A log counts as four planks** for every one of these, so a log makes a door straight away (two logs for a chest, with the spare planks handed back). A log can also be **stripped** (one log, one stripped log) or sawn into four planks.

The Saw tab reads the recipe book, so a modded recipe made of planks and sticks joins the list. It does not do recipes that need anything else (a barrel's slabs, a ladder's sticks alone).

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); stone, ceramic, glass and crystal at the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md). A tool is put together here, by fitting parts to a part. The screen is oak planks and dark board.
