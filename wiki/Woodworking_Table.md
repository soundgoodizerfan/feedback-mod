# Woodworking Table

A block with no vanilla original.

## Crafting

A carpet (any wool carpet) on top of a Crafting Table.

## What it does

An **axe** in its own slot instead of a hammer (any axe, vanilla or modular; each act costs it a point). No hand saw or hand axe exists.

Every bench has the same screen: a bench top with a centre slot and a column of slots on the right (the hand tool, a stock slot, a plan slot and the result). Put something in the centre and the bench answers with a **fan** of floating nodes around it; drag to pan, scroll to zoom. There are no tabs: what is in the centre decides what the fan shows.

- **Stock** in the centre: the fan shows every shape that stock can become, as icons. Shapes the pile is too small for are faint. Hover one for a ghost of the part by the result slot and a few bars (hardness, toughness, weight); click it to make it. A part takes only the stock it needs and the rest comes back as smaller items: a block that makes a one-ingot part gives the part and eight more ingots (cobble, for stone). **Break down** turns a big piece (a block) into ingot-sized pieces without making anything.
- A **part** in the centre: the fan shows what is already fitted to it (click to take it off), then what you are carrying that attaches, then every part that could attach and would be shaped from the stock slot. Click a node to fit it. Fitting the last part makes the tool, which becomes the centre.
- A **tool** in the centre: the fan shows its parts. Click one to see what you carry that fits that place (swap it, add to an empty place, or mend a worn part with the same stuff) or to take it off; what is left is the part it was built from, with the rest fitted to it.

What the stock offers here:

- **Shapes:** parts from organic stock: wood, nether wood, bamboo, bone, leather, wool and fibre. Tool heads, wooden hammer heads and knife blades, armour plates, shield faces and guards all come from it. Nothing here is forged: a shape comes out as soon as you click it. The fan shows only what that stock can make: fabric offers bindings and glove bases and nothing else. Every wooden part can be made from one plank; a log or a plank counts as one ingot of stock.
- **Saw:** a second folder tab above the panel. Put planks or a log in the centre; the cuts appear in the fan, and the shapes stay on the **Parts** tab. It makes what a crafting grid makes from planks and sticks alone: stairs, slabs, doors, trapdoors, fences, signs, boats, chests, bowls, sticks and the like (a stick counts as half a plank). **A log counts as four planks** for every one of these, so a log makes a door straight away (two logs for a chest, with the spare planks handed back). A log can also be **stripped** (one log, one stripped log) or sawn into four planks.

The Saw tab reads the recipe book, so a modded recipe made of planks and sticks joins the list. It does not do recipes that need anything else (a barrel's slabs, a ladder's sticks alone).

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); stone, ceramic, glass and crystal at the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md). A tool is put together here, by fitting parts to a part. The screen is oak planks and dark board.
