# Woodworking Table

A new block with no vanilla original.

It shapes parts from organic stock: wood, nether wood, bamboo, bone, leather and fibre. Tool heads, armor, bowls and doors all come from it. Nothing here is forged: a shape comes out as soon as the stock is in. Put a [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) in the hammer slot.

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); stone, ceramic, glass and crystal at the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md). A tool is put together by laying its parts in a crafting grid.
