# Woodworking Table

A new block with no vanilla original.

## Crafting

A carpet (any wool carpet) on top of a Crafting Table.

## What it does

Two tabs, and an **axe** in its own slot instead of a hammer (any axe, vanilla or modular; each act costs it a point). No hand saw or hand axe exists.

- **Parts:** shapes parts from organic stock: wood, nether wood, bamboo, bone, leather, wool and fibre. Tool heads, wooden hammer heads and knife blades, armour plates, shield faces and guards all come from it. Nothing here is forged: a shape comes out as soon as the stock is in. Put stock in the cells and the list shows only what that stock can make: fabric offers bindings and glove bases and nothing else.
- **Saw:** put planks or a log in the first cell, pick a result. It makes what a crafting grid makes from planks and sticks alone: stairs, slabs, doors, trapdoors, fences, signs, boats, chests, bowls, sticks and the like (a stick counts as half a plank). **A log counts as four planks** for every one of these, so a log makes a door straight away (two logs for a chest, with the spare planks handed back). A log can also be **stripped** (one log, one stripped log) or sawn into four planks.

The Saw tab reads the recipe book, so a modded recipe made of planks and sticks joins the list. It does not do recipes that need anything else (a barrel's slabs, a ladder's sticks alone).

Metal parts are made at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md); stone, ceramic, glass and crystal at the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md). A tool is put together by laying its parts in a crafting grid. The screen is oak planks and dark board.
