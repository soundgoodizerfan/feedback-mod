# Fertilizer

A powder that pushes a growing [crop](Crops.md) on like bone meal, and scorches one that is already grown.

## Obtaining

Made in a [Reactor](Reactor.md) from 200 mB [ammonia](Chemicals.md#ammonia) and 100 mB [sulfuric acid](Chemicals.md#acids): two Fertilizer per batch.

## Using it

Right-click a crop with it.

- **Growing crop.** It grows as with bone meal.
- **Fully grown crop.** It scorches: the crop is destroyed with a puff of smoke and drops nothing. Harvest first.
