# Fertilizer

A powder that feeds one [crop](Crops.md): it grows about three times as fast and gives about half again as much when harvested ripe, until it is harvested.

## Obtaining

Made in a [Reactor](Reactor.md) from 200 mB [ammonia](Chemicals.md#ammonia) and 100 mB of [sulfuric or hydrochloric acid](Chemicals.md#acids): two Fertilizer per batch.

## Using it

Right-click a crop block with it. One Fertilizer is used per dose.

- **Unfed crop, young or ripe.** It is fed. Green sparkles show it, and an occasional sparkle keeps showing while the crop grows.
- **Crop that is still fed.** A second dose scorches it: the crop is destroyed with a puff of smoke and drops nothing.

## How it works

- **Growth.** A fed crop gets about three times as many growth chances as an unfed one. Light, moisture and the [crop climate](Crops.md) still decide each chance, so a fed crop in the cold or dark grows slowly.
- **Yield.** Harvested ripe, each drop (produce and seeds) comes out about half again as large, rounded at random. A crop broken before it is ripe gives no bonus.
- **Duration.** The feeding lasts until that crop is harvested or broken, and survives saving and reloading. A replanted crop is unfed.
- **Bone meal still works** on a fed crop.

## Compared with bone meal and Green Thumb

| | Bone meal | Fertilizer | Green thumb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | One block | One crop block | A wide area around the drinker |
| Duration | Instant | Until harvest | While the effect lasts |
| Overdose | Safe | A second dose scorches the crop | None |

Bone meal is the quick, safe nudge. Fertilizer is the lasting boost for one crop. [Green thumb](Brewing_Stand.md#potion-effects) is the wide burst tied to the drinker, also thrown or [imbued](Thrown_and_Imbued_Potions.md).
