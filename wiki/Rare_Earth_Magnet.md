# Rare-Earth Magnet

A strong permanent magnet, used as the core of a [Dynamotor](Dynamotor.md). As a core it wastes far less of the power through it as heat than iron does.

## Making it

1. **Blend** iron with Rare Earth Residue (the leach residue of monazite), **22 to 38 percent** residue, in the [Mixer](Mixer.md).
2. **Press** the powder into a green compact.
3. **Sinter** the compact in a vessel at **1000 to 1120 Tu** under argon. Too hot or no argon gives Porous Sinter instead. See [Furnaces](Furnaces.md#sintering).
4. **Magnetize** the sintered blank: hold it near a full-strength [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and it becomes a Rare-Earth Magnet ingot.

## Using it

Use the ingot as the core in the Dynamotor's tool-table build.
