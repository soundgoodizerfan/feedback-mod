# Sawblade

A bare blade, with no handle, that goes in the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md)'s saw slot. The stonecutter does nothing without one.

## Crafting

Cast a **Saw Blade** in a [Saw Blade Mold](Casting.md#tool-part-molds) from one ingot's worth of any metal the mold takes, or shape one from a metal plate, then assemble it at a bench as described in [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md#putting-one-together). The blade's material sets how long the sawblade lasts.

## Using it

Put the sawblade in the saw slot of the Stonecutter. Every act there costs it a point of wear: shaping a part, breaking stock down, or a cut on the Cut tab (stone cuts, and wood cuts when a plank or log is in the center). With the slot empty the screen reads "Put a sawblade in the saw slot."
