# Aluminum

Aluminum is a light, soft metal that conducts well for its weight. It is never smelted from its ore: bauxite is dissolved in lye and the alumina is split by electrolysis. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

Bauxite (40% aluminum, Overworld y 20 to 90) and cryolite ore (10%, y -20 to 60) are the ores ([Ores](Ores.md)). Bauxite melted in a crucible does not give aluminum. The route is lye, then alumina, then a molten cryolite bath in an [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md#aluminum-bath), which gives 72 mB of molten aluminum per alumina. The full path and its failure points are [Guide: Making Aluminum](Guide_Making_Aluminum.md).

## Figures

| Figure | Aluminum |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 2.5 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 2.7 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 660 |
| Toughness | 30 |
| Yield strength (St) | 2 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 23 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0591 |
| Conductivity | 0.61 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.8 |

Aluminum weighs about a third as much as iron and conducts current about six tenths as well as copper, so it carries a lot of current for its weight. It is soft and weak.

## What it is used in

- **Heatsinks.** Aluminum Plates make the Heatsink of the [Solid-State Converter](Converter.md#heatsinks), and plates also go into the Converter and its tap.
- **Gas cells.** Eight Aluminum Plates in a ring make two Gas Cell plates ([Gas Cell](Gas_Cell.md)), and a plate patches a hole in a cell.
- **Reducing vanadinite.** Aluminum nuggets, scrap or swarf reduce vanadinite concentrate to vanadium ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning)).
- **Alloys.** Duralumin, alnico, titanium 6Al-4V and aluminum foam ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys), [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 2 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
