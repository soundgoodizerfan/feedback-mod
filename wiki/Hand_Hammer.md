# Hand Hammer

A stone hammer you swing yourself, in a crafting grid. It's the first way to make plates, before you have any machines, and it teaches you the mod's main lesson the hard way.

## Crafting

```
S S S
S T S
  T
```

S is Cobblestone and T is a Stick.

## Using it

Put the hammer and **one** workpiece anywhere in a crafting grid. The output slot shows what that piece will be **after one more blow**. Take it, and that's one swing: the hammer loses a little durability and the piece takes one blow's worth of work.

Keep swinging and watch the result change. A copper ingot takes several blows to become a **Copper Plate**. Keep going and the plate becomes **Copper Foil**, then **Copper Scrap**.

> **Careful with shift-click.** Shift-clicking swings until the material runs out, which takes an ingot straight past plate and foil and into scrap. That's the mod's core idea in miniature: whatever is doing the hitting doesn't know when to stop. See [Metalworking](Metalworking.md).

## What it can work

It's a light blow. It can shape soft metals: **copper, tin, zinc, gold and lead**. It can also hammer **nuggets into bullets** (see [Ammunition](Ammunition.md)).

It does **nothing** to harder metals like brass, bronze, iron or steel. The craft simply isn't offered. For those you need a [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md) with the right gearing.

Very soft metals like lead move a lot with each blow, so it's easy to overshoot with them.

## Wear

Unlike machines, the hand hammer wears out from **normal use**, like any hand tool. Each swing costs durability, and eventually it breaks. They're cheap.
