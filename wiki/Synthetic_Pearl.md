# Synthetic Pearl

A pearl made from glass, metal and [Liquid Teleportant](Liquid_Teleportant.md). It does everything an Ender Pearl does: it makes a bonded [Ender Port](Ender_Port.md) pair, tunes a [Tuned Eye](Tuned_Eye.md) and gives the blink perk.

## Making it

Heat in a vessel at **1100 to 1500 Tu**, best at 1250 Tu: one Silica Glass ingot, one Redstone Chromium ingot and **250 mB** of Liquid Teleportant. See [Materials](Materials.md#silica-glass-and-optical-glass) for the glass.
