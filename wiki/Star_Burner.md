# Star Burner

A heat source that burns a Nether Star. Set against a [Crucible](Crucible.md) or another vessel, it heats it hotter than an arc can hold, and it cannot be switched off.

## Crafting

```
T S T
C   C
T C T
```

T is a Tungsten Carbide Plate, S is a Steel Plate and C is Ceramic. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row, 4 machine bolts ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)) and Nuclear Treatise I: Reactors and Fuel ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

The burner faces the block it was placed against and heats it, like an [Arc Head](Arc_Furnace.md#the-arc-head): it heats that block's face and all four sides of it ([Heat](Heat.md#heat-sources-have-a-facing)). Set it on top of a crucible for the usual build.

- **Right-click with a Nether Star** to seat it. The burner holds one.
- **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the star out. The star is as hot as the furnace it feeds, so taking it burns your hand ([Hazards](Hazards.md)).

Breaking the burner drops the star.

## How it works

A seated star heats the vessel at a constant temperature above the arc's cap, enough to melt [tungsten](Tungsten.md). It needs no cable, fuel or power, and nothing dims it: the heat stays until the star is taken out or spent. The vessel climbs toward that temperature as it does under any fire.

The star decays very slowly, about 20 hours of loaded time. When it is spent the burner goes cold and is empty.
