# Relics

A relic is a part that is its own thing: it has no material to pick, and its properties are fixed. Four sword blades are relics, and they come with the Aether mod.

## Obtaining

A relic blade is a **Sword Blade** like any other, but it is made of one named thing instead of a metal. It needs no [blueprint](Blueprint_Table.md) and can always be crafted.

Engineering Table grids; S is a plate of hardness 14 or more.

```
Holy Sword        Flaming Sword     Lightning Sword
 V                 S                 C
GSG               SRS               SCS
 S                 S                 S
```

V is a silver plate, G a gold plate, R a blaze rod and C a copper plate. The Candy Cane Sword is five sugar in a plus shape in the [Crafting Table](Crafting_Table.md).

| Relic | Bench | Perk |
|---|---|---|
| Holy Sword | [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) | **Smite** |
| Flaming Sword | [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) | **Fire aspect** |
| Lightning Sword | [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) | **Arc** |
| Candy Cane Sword | [Crafting Table](Crafting_Table.md) | **Cling** |

The three Engineering Table recipes need a hammer and a file in the hand tool slots and the Weapons [treatise](Treatise.md) held. The perks are described in [Material Perks](Material_Perks.md).

A found Aether sword of any of the four kinds turns into a modular sword with the relic blade, the way [old vanilla tools](Tools_and_Weapons.md#old-vanilla-tools) do. All four blades are in the Parts tab of the creative inventory.

## Using it

A relic blade goes into a sword like any blade; the sword takes its perk from the blade. The blade has no material slot to change, so a blueprint for it shows no material stepper.

## Mending and scrap

Mending a relic blade repairs it and never melts it. Scrapping one gives nothing back.
