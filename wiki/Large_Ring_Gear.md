# Large Ring Gear

A [Ring Gear](Ring_Gear.md) with the teeth of a large cog. A small cog driving it turns what it carries at half the speed a small Ring Gear would turn it.

## Crafting

```
  C
CBC
  C
```

C is a Large Cog and B is a [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md). The corners are empty.

## Using it

It is placed, assembled and put back the way a Ring Gear is: build in front of it and right-click with an empty hand. It can also be a voxel of a [micro build](Micro_Core.md). The messages, the size limit and the rules for putting back are the [Rotary Bearing's](Rotary_Bearing.md).

## How it works

Its rim meshes with cogs as a Large Cog's does. A Small Cog driving it turns it at half the speed a small Ring Gear would turn; a Large Cog beside it turns it as it would turn another large cog. See [Cogs](Cogs.md#how-cogs-work) for the meshing rules.
