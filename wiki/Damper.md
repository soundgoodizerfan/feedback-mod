# Damper

A vent flap that you build against a [Crucible](Crucible.md). Open, it **lets heat out**. It's the opposite of the [Powered Bellows](Powered_Bellows.md).

## Using it

Place it against the crucible's side, the same way as [Insulation](Insulation.md). Up to **four** count. Each open damper lets out **twice** what a bare crucible loses on its own, on top of whatever insulation is doing.

- **Right-click** to open or close it.
- A [Timer](Timer.md), [Bimetallic Strip](Bimetallic_Strip.md) or [Controller](Controller.md) can open and close it for you.

## Why you'd want one

The bellows can only make things **hotter**. Without a damper, the only way to cool a crucible is to stop heating it and wait, and a big insulated crucible waits a long time.

With dampers you can:

- **Cool on demand**, so you don't overshoot.
- **Temper steel**, which only counts while the metal is **cooling** through its band. Heat past the band, then open the dampers and let it fall through slowly. See [Heat Treatment](Heat_Treatment.md).

A damper is open or shut, nothing in between. To cool slowly, open and close it in a rhythm (by hand, or with a controller).
