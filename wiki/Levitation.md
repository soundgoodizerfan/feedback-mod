# Levitation

Diamagnetic materials are pushed away by a powered [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md). The **Levitation Plate** floats a vehicle or makes a slippery floor; the **Levitation Sleeve** cuts the drag of a turning part. How strongly a material answers is its diamagnetism ([Materials](Materials.md#the-metals)); bismuth is the strongest.

## Levitation Plate

A 4/16 slab of a diamagnetic material that a powered [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) holds off. On the ground it makes a slippery surface; inside a [vehicle](Vehicles.md) it floats.

### Crafting

Two plates of the same diamagnetic material side by side in the crafting grid make one Levitation Plate in that material. See [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals) for the rest.

### Using it

Place it up to 3 blocks above a powered Electromagnet (center to center). Jade shows *Held off by a field* while the magnet holds it and *Resting* otherwise.

### How it works

- **Slippery surface.** The plate's surface friction is a block's ordinary 0.6 over no magnet. Over a powered magnet it rises toward ice's 0.98, more for a stronger field and a higher diamagnetism of its material.
- **Weight.** A plate weighs what its material weighs, set against bismuth. A denser plate pulls its body down harder and needs more lift. It weighs the same with no magnet under it.
- **Floating.** Inside a vehicle body (made on the [Lift](Vehicles.md#the-lift)) each plate is lifted by a springy force. At full field and full diamagnetism, with the plate touching the magnet, the lift is twice the plate's own weight, and it falls steadily to nothing 3 blocks up. A bare plate settles about a block above the magnet; a load pressed on it sits lower. The plate bounces a little before it settles. Sparks show while it floats.

## Levitation Sleeve

A sleeve of diamagnetic material that cuts the drag of a [Shaft](Shaft.md), [Flywheel](Flywheel.md) or [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md) while a powered [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) touches it.

### Crafting

One plate and one rod of the same diamagnetic material, anywhere in the grid, make one Levitation Sleeve in that material. See [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals) for the diamagnetic materials.

### Using it

Use it on a shaft, flywheel or rotary bearing to fit it. Each holds one sleeve; breaking the block drops the sleeve. Jade shows *Cushioned by a field* while a powered magnet touches the block and *Dry* otherwise.

### How it works

While a powered Electromagnet touches the fitted block, its drag falls by up to 90 percent. A free rotary bearing's damping falls the same way. The cut is smaller for a weaker field and for a material of lower diamagnetism.
