# Instrument Bag

A bag for your [instruments](Home.md#a-few-things-worth-knowing). Instruments read from your inventory, so a pack with eight of them spends eight slots on things nobody holds. The bag keeps up to **6 instruments** in one slot, and they **still read**: Jade and tooltips look inside it as they look along your hotbar. It is not an instrument itself and has no figures of its own.

## Crafting

```
S L S
L _ L
L L L
```

S is String and L is Leather.

## Using it

- Click an instrument onto the bag (or the bag onto an instrument) with a **right-click** to stow it.
- Right-click the bag on an empty slot, or on its own slot with an empty cursor, to take the last one stowed back out.

Only instruments go in ([Calipers](Calipers.md), [Balance Scale](Balance_Scale.md), [Scratch Files](Scratch_Files.md), [Thermometer](Thermometer.md), [Electroscope](Electroscope.md), [Galvanometer](Galvanometer.md) and the like). The limit is a count, not a weight.
