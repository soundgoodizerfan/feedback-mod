# Bronze

Bronze is copper hardened with tin: much harder and stronger than either metal alone, and the first metal good enough for shafts, bearings and machine plates. It belongs to the Bronze [age](Ages.md).

## Making it

Melt copper and tin into the same [Crucible](Crucible.md) or [furnace](Furnaces.md) in the right proportions. The mixture has to land inside the window:

| Metal | Share of the melt |
| --- | --- |
| [Copper](Copper.md) | 88 to 92% |
| [Tin](Tin.md) | 8 to 12% |

About 9 parts copper to 1 part tin sits in the middle. Outside the window the tank looks empty and nothing pours; add whichever metal is short. JEI's *Mixing* card shows the window. See [Casting and Alloys](Casting.md#alloys).

## Figures

| Figure | Bronze |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 10 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 8.8 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1000 |
| Toughness | 50 |
| Yield strength (St) | 10 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 18 |
| Heat conduction | 0.0137 |
| Conductivity | 0.15 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.8 |

Bronze is more than twice as hard as copper, with nearly three times its yield strength, at half the toughness. A bronze edge mines gold, diamond and redstone ore. It conducts far worse than copper.

## What it is used in

- **Shafts.** Three Bronze Ingots in a line make four [Shafts](Shaft.md).
- **Machine plates.** Bronze Plates go into the [Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md), the [Clutch](Clutch.md), the [Damper](Damper.md), the [Crank Linkage](Crank_Linkage.md), the [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md), the [Timer](Timer.md) and the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md).
- **Cartridge cases.** A bronze case holds 7.5 Pu ([Ammunition](Ammunition.md#cases)); a bronze barrel chamber rates like wrought iron ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber)). A bronze grip is slippery, so a gun kicks harder in the hand ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#grip-friction)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 2 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).
- **Amethyst bronze.** Bronze with a little amethyst, the best conductor of experience ([Experience](Experience.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
