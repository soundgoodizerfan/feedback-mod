# Elytra

A vanilla glider worn on the chest. Feedback makes it craftable.

## What vanilla does

Elytra drop only from End ships, and a firework rocket boosts a glide.

## What Feedback does

Elytra are crafted from five Shulker Shells and three Phantom Membranes:

```
M S M
S M S
S   S
```

S is a [Shulker Shell](Vanilla_Changes.md#shulker) and M is a Phantom Membrane. Gliding works as in vanilla, but a firework rocket used while gliding does nothing and stays in your hand.

A wing lifts by the air under it. In thin air, such as the End's, a glide sinks faster the thinner the air is, so there it is a controlled descent rather than a free glide.

A wing carries its glider. Armour is carried on it: the heavier the armour, the faster a glide sinks. The phantom membrane in the wing tears only when the load passes what it can take, a heavy suit of armour, and then the elytra wears faster the further over it is. A worn elytra lifts less, so it sinks a little more the more damaged it is.
