# Elytra

A vanilla glider worn on the chest. Feedback makes it craftable.

## What vanilla does

Elytra drop only from End ships, and a firework rocket boosts a glide.

## What Feedback does

Elytra are crafted from five Shulker Shells and three Phantom Membranes:

```
M S M
S M S
S   S
```

S is a [Shulker Shell](Shulker.md) and M is a Phantom Membrane. Gliding works as in vanilla, but a firework rocket used while gliding does nothing and stays in your hand.
