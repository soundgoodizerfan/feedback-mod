# Anvil

A vanilla block. Feedback keeps the block and makes it the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md)'s upgrade: it shapes metal the same way and also **forges**.

## What vanilla does

It repairs, renames and combines tools and armor, spending experience levels. It wears down through chipped and damaged stages and breaks when it falls.

## Crafting

```
W W W
W S W
W W W
```

W is a Wrought Iron Plate and S is a [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md). The tooltip reads "Also forges! A Hearth beside it keeps the stock hot." Mine it with a pickaxe: without one it drops nothing.

## What Feedback does

The Anvil does everything the Smithing Table does: the same metal stock, the **Forge** and **Trim** tabs, and a [Hand Hammer](Hand_Hammer.md) in the hammer slot. See the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) for the screen. On top of that it makes the **forged** variants of a part, played as a rhythm. Vanilla's repair, rename and combine do not exist here; every damage stage of a placed or generated vanilla anvil is this block.

The Anvil has vanilla's shape and turns to face the way you place it. A [Hearth](Hearth.md) set beside the Anvil keeps its stock hot while you play. The Smithing Table is not reheated.

## Forging

A forged variant is played in the Anvil's screen: put the stock in the center and click the variant in the fan. **Tap** on the left lane (left click or Z) and **heavy** on the right (right click or X) as notes fall to the ring. A metronome clicks the tempo and counts four beats in. Each blow flashes Clean, Loose or Miss; a harder metal plays slower. The stock cools while you play: notes struck out of its window are dead. A sloppy run leaves **flaws** that take a share of the edge's toughness and durability. Players who learned Cadence ([Onslaught](Skills.md#onslaught)) see tempo pips under the beat: clean blows in a row light them, and each lit pip makes the next blows land a little more Work. The screen is a cold cast-iron panel with polished-steel trim, and the bench rings like an anvil. The pattern is played around the stock on the bench; nothing covers the screen.

Which variants exist for each part is listed under [Forging variants by rhythm](Tools_and_Weapons.md#forging-variants-by-rhythm).
