# Hearth

A brick pan that keeps the [Tool Table](Tool_Table.md) and the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) hot. Stock cooling in the table's cells while you play a long forging pattern is the problem it solves.

## Crafting

```
B   B
B B B
```

B is a Brick.

## Using it

Set it **on top of a [Firebox](Firebox.md)** (or any fire below it, lava included) and put a **Tool Table against one of its sides**. Light the fire. The pan warms toward the flame and never passes it: open to the air it settles a little under the fire's heat, and a [Bellows](Powered_Bellows.md) on the firebox raises it as it raises any vessel.

While the pan is hot, hot-working stock lying in the table's cells (steel and the like) is pulled toward the pan's heat, a few percent of the gap each tick, so cold stock warms quickly and warm stock barely at all. It cools the other way as ever, so stock settles a little under the pan and never over it. Planks, stone and other cold-worked stock are not heated.

- Stock left in too long can get hotter than the metal's working window; the table then refuses it until it cools.
- It has no screen and holds nothing. It wears from nothing.
- A hot pan burns a bare hand like any hot block: wear [gloves](Hazards.md#gloves) or leave it alone.
