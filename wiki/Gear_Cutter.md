# Gear Cutter

Cuts a metal plate into a cog of that metal, with the tooth count you choose. It is how [cogs](Cogs.md) of different ratios are made.

The first part cut on a gear cutter enters the [Machine Age](Ages.md) for you and any player nearby.

## Crafting

```
  W
I S I
I B I
```

W is a Saw Head, I is a Steel Plate, S is a [Shaft](Shaft.md) and B is a [Bearing](Bearing.md). Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 machine bolts ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)). It needs Mechanical Treatise I: Engines, Flywheels and Dynamotors and Materials Treatise I: Machine-Shop Consumables ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

1. Put it on a shaft line (a shaft stub shows on each side; any side couples) and get it turning. It's a **30 Su** load.
2. **Sneak-right-click with an empty hand** to open the tooth screen and pick **8, 12, 16, 24 or 32** teeth for the next cog. The screen shows the count the next cut gives.
3. **Right-click with a metal plate** to lay it on the table.
4. **Right-click with an empty hand** to make one cut, or put a [Crank Linkage](Crank_Linkage.md) in front of it and each stroke makes one. The wheel has to be turning; a cut on a stopped wheel does nothing.
5. **Right-click with an item** on an occupied table to take the piece back out: the plate before a cut, the cog after.

## What it makes

**Plate → Cog**

The cog is of the plate's metal, with the tooth count the cutter is set to. It is a Small Cog at 8 or 12 teeth and a Large Cog at 16, 24 or 32 ([Cogs](Cogs.md)). Every count shares one pitch, so any two cogs mesh at the ratio of their teeth.

The metal that is not left on the cog falls out of the top as swarf. A cog with fewer teeth leaves more swarf than one with more.

## Wear

A plate harder than the wheel can bite wears the machine and gives nothing. Softer metals cut cleanly. A heavier metal makes a heavier cog to spin up.
