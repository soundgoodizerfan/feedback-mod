# Spatula

A modular hand tool for flipping the food in a [Skillet](Skillet.md).

## Crafting

```
P
T
```

P is a wrought iron plate and T is a Stick. This gives a wrought iron blade on a wooden handle.

To choose other materials, shape a **Spatula Blade** (from a plate, planks or bone) and put it with a handle (stick or bone) at the Tool Table, as for any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md).

## Using it

Right-click a piece in a skillet to flip it. The quarter under your crosshair is the one that turns. Only the face down chars, and a flip swaps the faces.

Each flip wears the spatula one point. To lift the pan off the fire, use [Tongs](Hazards.md#lifting-a-skillet).
