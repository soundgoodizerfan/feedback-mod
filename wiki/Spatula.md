# Spatula

A modular hand tool for working a [Skillet](Skillet.md): flip the food in it, or lift the whole pan off the fire.

## Crafting

```
P
T
```

P is a wrought iron plate and T is a Stick. This gives a wrought iron blade on a wooden handle.

To choose other materials, shape a **Spatula Blade** (from a plate, planks or bone) and put it with a handle (stick or bone) at the Tool Table, as for any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md).

## Using it

Aim at the pan from up to 4.5 blocks and use the spatula (right-click).

- **Tap** (a quick click, under about a third of a second): flips the piece you are aiming at. Only the face down chars, and a flip swaps the faces.
- **Hold:** lifts the pan off the fire while you keep looking at it. The pan cools toward room temperature. Release to set it back on the fire.

## Wear and heat

Holding a pan up wears the spatula one point every 5 seconds. A hot pan heats your hand through a metal handle; a wooden handle insulates. Past what the handle holds, you take the usual [hot contact](Hazards.md) burn.
