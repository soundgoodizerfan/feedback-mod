# Spatula

A modular hand tool for flipping the food in a [Skillet](Skillet.md).

## Crafting

Shape a **Spatula Blade** (from a plate, planks or bone; metal is shaped hot at the [Smithing Table](Smithing_Table.md) or [Anvil](Anvil.md)) and put it with a handle (stick or bone) in a crafting grid, as for any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md).

The blade's shape is the choice:

| Blade | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Turner** | Turns one piece. |
| **Slotted Turner** | Turns one piece and lets about a tenth of its moisture run off through the slots. |

## Using it

Right-click a piece in a skillet to flip it. The quarter under your crosshair is the one that turns. Only the face down chars, and a flip swaps the faces.

Each flip wears the spatula one point. To lift the pan off the fire, use [Tongs](Hazards.md#lifting-a-skillet).
