# Balance Scale

Tells you exactly how heavy a [tool, weapon or piece of armor](Tools_and_Weapons.md) is. The tooltip already says *head-heavy* or *heavy* for free; the scale says how many.

## Crafting

```
  R
I   I
I I I
```

R is a Stick and I is a Wrought Iron Ingot.

## Using it

**Carry it anywhere in your inventory.** Every modular item's tooltip gains a line like *"Balance scale: 3.4 Mu"*. It reads to a tenth.

Mass matters because a heavier head hits harder and swings slower, and because a head that's too heavy for its handle works the joint loose.
