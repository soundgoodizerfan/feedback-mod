# Redstone Lamp

## What vanilla does

The Redstone Lamp is a block that lights up while it is powered by a redstone signal, and goes dark when the signal stops. It is crafted from glowstone and redstone dust.

## What Feedback does

The Redstone Lamp is replaced by the [Glowstone Lamp](Lamp.md). It has no crafting recipe, and Redstone Lamps already in a world turn into Glowstone Lamps.

The Glowstone Lamp is a load on a power line, not a block watching a redstone signal: it takes 0.5 A at 6 V, glows in four steps by how well the line feeds it, dims when starved and burns out if given twice its rated voltage. See [Electricity](Electricity.md).
