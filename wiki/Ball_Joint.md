# Ball Joint

A micro-block joint that lets one body swing in every direction inside a cone. It is the joint for a suspension arm, a turret or a leg.

## Crafting

```
I I
 B 
I I
```

I is a Wrought Iron Plate and B is a [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md).

## Using it

It works only as a [micro-block](Micro_Core.md). Placed as a full-size block it says *"It only works as a micro-block: put it in a micro cell."*

1. Put it in a micro cell with its front toward the part that should swing.
2. **Sneak-use** it with an empty hand to choose the cone: **narrow** (20 degrees), **stock** (45 degrees) or **wide** (90 degrees). The message reads *"Choice N of 3."* Choose before assembling.
3. **Use it with an empty hand** to assemble everything joined to its front face into a second body that swings about the joint.
4. **Use it again** to put the body back. It lands at the nearest of the 24 ways a cube can be turned.

The body swings freely inside the cone and is stopped at its edge. Put back the outermost body first when joints are chained. A [Lift](Vehicles.md#the-lift) carries the swung body with the vehicle and keeps its angle.
