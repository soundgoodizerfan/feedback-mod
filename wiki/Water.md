# Water

A vanilla fluid. Feedback keeps everything it does and adds one thing for hot work.

## What vanilla does

It extinguishes fire, carries items and mobs along, lets you swim, and fills cauldrons and farmland.

## What Feedback does

Everything above still works. On top of it, water is how you **cool a workpiece by hand**:

- **Right-click a water source** with a hot piece in hand and it snaps to room temperature.
- **Drop a hot piece in water** and it is cooled instantly. For hot steel this is [quenching](Heat_Treatment.md).

Room temperature depends on the dimension: see [Heat](Heat.md#room-temperature).
