# Galvanometer

A needle that swings when current runs through it. It tells you how much current is actually flowing through an [electrical](Electricity.md) block.

## Crafting

```
W W W
N G N
```

W is [Copper Wire](Wire_Drawer.md), N is an Iron Nugget and G is a Glass Pane.

## Using it

- **Right-click** any electrical block to read the current on your action bar, e.g. *"Galvanometer: 1.00 A"*.
- On a **machine**, it shows what the machine is getting next to what it wants, e.g. *"0.50 of 1.00 A"*. That's the quickest way to tell a starved machine from one that's simply weak.
- **Carry it anywhere in your inventory** and Jade's tooltip on electrical blocks gains an amps line.

Reading a live cable is free and safe. Nothing has to stop while you measure.

## What it's good for

- Finding which cable on a busy line is carrying the most (and so is about to overheat).
- Checking whether adding another [Galvanic Cell](Galvanic_Cell.md) actually helped.
- Working out why a magnet is weak: low amps means not enough supply, while full amps on a weak magnet means the voltage is the problem (long run). Check that with an [Electroscope](Electroscope.md).
