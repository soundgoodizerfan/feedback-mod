# Grapple

A hook on a line. Throw it at a block or a creature and the line catches: a block, or a creature heavier than you, holds you on the line and you **swing** from it like a pendulum. A lighter creature is the one that moves, hauled in to you.

## Crafting

```
P R _
_ R S
_ _ S
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate, R a Wrought Iron Rod and S String. Recipe appears once you have wrought iron plate. It has 240 durability.

## Using it

Use it to throw. The hook flies fast on a short arc and bites the first block or living thing in its path. The line is up to **24 blocks** long.

- **A block, or a creature heavier than you:** the line is fixed at the bite. Its length is how far you were from the anchor when it caught, up to 24 blocks. Nothing pulls you in. Fall, or run past the line's length, and you arc around the anchor, keeping your sideways speed. The swing is a smooth pendulum; a hook straight overhead does not bounce.
- **A creature lighter than you:** it is dragged to you. Mass is judged by body size, the same stand-in as the [parry](Combat.md#parry)'s.
- **Release:** sneak, or use the grapple again. The line also lets go if it parts, if it has been out for ten seconds, or if either end is gone.

A [Tether](Combat.md#tethers) holds the body on the line, and a creature held that way takes extra damage as with a harpoon.

## The line has a rating

The line's material (string, wool's strength) sets the load it can take. The load is the mass of the body on the line. A creature being hauled in counts as far heavier on any tick its reel stalls (caught on a corner, held by the ground). While you swing, your own weight is the load, so a heavy player on a weak line can part it. Over the rating the line gains strain every tick and **parts at full strain**; a heavier overload parts it sooner. The grapple takes 20 wear when the line parts. A load under the rating never strains it, and wear is for misuse: a pull or swing under the rating costs nothing.
