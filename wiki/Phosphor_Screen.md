# Phosphor Screen

A sealed glass envelope coated inside with glowstone dust, which glows where a signal lands. Pair it with an [Oscilloscope](Oscilloscope.md).

## Crafting

A Glass and two Glowstone Dust, in any order.

## Sealing it

Set it on a [Firebox](Firebox.md) and let it heat. **Right-click with an empty hand** to pinch the envelope off. The gas sealed inside contracts as it cools, which makes a partial vacuum: **the hotter you seal it, the better the vacuum**. A [Pressure Gauge](Pressure_Gauge.md) reads it (negative is vacuum). It glows only when it is sealed and the gauge reads below about -0.3 Pu.

## Using it

Put an [Oscilloscope](Oscilloscope.md) against it. The screen has four brightness levels and gives light to match.

## Overrun

- **Reheating a sealed envelope** past the heat it was sealed at turns the vacuum into pressure. Above a small pressure it may shatter, certainly if pushed far, and past the glass's service temperature it cracks outright. Nothing is returned.
- **Burn-in**: a screen held at full brightness slowly burns, and each third of burn costs the brightest level it can show, for good. Do not leave a maxed signal on it.
