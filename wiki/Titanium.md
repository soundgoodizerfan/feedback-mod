# Titanium

Titanium is a light, strong, corrosion-proof metal that stands a high heat. Its ore cannot be melted to metal, so it comes only from a chemical reduction. It belongs to the Alloy [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

Its ore is ilmenite, which lies in the Nether from y 10 to 117 ([Ores](Ores.md)). Ilmenite is **refractory**: a crucible or furnace never melts it to metal. Two routes give titanium ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#reducing-refractory-ores)):

1. **Kroll.** Two magnesium ingots and 144 mB titanium tetrachloride, held at 800 to 1050 Tu (best near 900), give 9 titanium nuggets.
2. **Sulfate.** Leach ilmenite concentrate with sulfuric acid, then precipitate the titanium with hydrogen.

## Figures

| Figure | Titanium |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 15 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 4.51 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1668 |
| Toughness | 55 |
| Yield strength (St) | 20 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 8.6 |
| Heat conduction | 0.00549 |
| Conductivity | 0.031 |
| Corrosion resistance | 1 |

Titanium is as hard as steel and stronger, at little more than half the weight, and it does not corrode. It conducts heat and current poorly and expands little when warmed. It also **soaks up hydrogen** and gives it back when hot.

## What it is used in

- **Hydrogen storage.** A titanium ingot loads a [Hydride Bed](Hydride_Bed.md#how-it-works).
- **Alloys.** Titanium 6Al-4V with aluminum and vanadium, aluminum foam with a little titanium, and titanium carbide, the grain of cermet ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys), [Materials](Materials.md#the-metals)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 5, the best ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
