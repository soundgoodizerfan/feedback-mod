# Interferometer

A hand instrument that counts light fringes to read a length a hundred times finer than [Calipers](Instruments.md#calipers). It reads length and nothing else.

## Crafting

```
L   L
P T P
  W
```

L is an Optical Glass Lens, P is a Steel Plate, T is Glass Tubing and W is Copper Fine Wire. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 2 screws. It needs Electrical Treatise IV: Lasers ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

Carry it anywhere in your inventory: every length a part shows prints to 0.001 mm, where calipers print to 0.1 mm. It has no battery and no screen of its own. Like any [instrument](Instruments.md) it unlocks nothing; it lets you read, and so aim at, a tolerance the shop could not otherwise check.

A [Laser](Laser.md) firing over a made part stamps its axes exact without any instrument.
