# Heavy Water

A scarce fluid left over when enriched water is electrolysed. A few mB of it damp [Unstable Spatial Solvent](Liquid_Teleportant.md#making-it) on the way to Liquid Teleportant.

## Making it

1. **Alkaline Electrolyte.** A [Reactor](Reactor.md#the-reactions) mixes 500 mB of water with 10 mB of [lye](Chemicals.md#lye) into 500 mB of Alkaline Electrolyte. Hold it between 0 and 80 Tu, fastest at 20.
2. **Enriched Water.** Run Alkaline Electrolyte through an [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md#what-it-splits) at 1.8 V. It gives hydrogen and oxygen, and about 1 mB of Enriched Water stays behind for every 25 mB split.
3. **Heavy Water.** Run Enriched Water through the Electrolyser at 1.8 V. It gives hydrogen and oxygen, and about 1 mB of Heavy Water stays behind for every 20 mB split.

Draw the residue off the cell like the lye from brine. A great deal of water goes through for a little Heavy Water.

## Using it

Reactors take it from a pipe or bucket as an input: 5 mB damp 100 mB of solvent ([Liquid Teleportant](Liquid_Teleportant.md#making-it)).
