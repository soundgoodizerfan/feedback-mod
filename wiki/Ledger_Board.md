# Ledger Board

The village's notice board. It shows every open order around it, the contracts the village posts, and notices about standing, market day and raids.

## Obtaining

Ledger Boards stand in the plazas of generated villages (see [Village Stalls](Village_Stalls.md)). A broken board drops itself and can be placed anywhere.

## Using it

Right-click opens three tabs.

### Orders

Every open order of the villagers within 48 blocks, grouped by type of piece. Deliveries still happen at the villager; the board is the overview.

### Contracts

The village posts contracts: a number of pieces of one kind, each at least **Good**, open for 3 days. Put a piece in the slot, pick the contract and press **Deliver**. Each piece pays by its grade. Finishing a contract adds a bonus and village goodwill. A blueprint comes with it when your pieces average Exceptional, or when the village honours you.

A [Counter](Counter.md) within 12 blocks of the board fills contracts from what reaches its intake.

### Notices

- Your standing tier with the village and what the next tier gives: more contracts each day, then a bigger bonus for finishing, then a blueprint for every contract.
- The market countdown (see [Market Cross](Market_Cross.md)).
- A raid warning.
- The banner bounty.

The village's standing is the mean of its villagers' positive gossip about you.

## Raids

Raiders in a raid head for Counters whose shelf holds 20 or more emeralds' worth, take the most valuable stack and drop it where they die. Guard stocked shelves.

A won raid leaves the village owing one **banner bounty**: put an illager banner in the contracts slot and press **Banner** for 16 emeralds. Villagers also praise the player with Hero of the Village.
