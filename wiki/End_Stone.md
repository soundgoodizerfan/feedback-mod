# End Stone

A vanilla block. Feedback gives it a second use as a source of hydrogen.

## What vanilla does

A pale, hard block found on the End island. It is a building block.

## What Feedback does

End stone heated in a [Reactor](Reactor.md#the-reactions) at **700 to 1300 Tu** releases **250 mB of hydrogen** and leaves **Porous Aggregate**, a very light rock that barely conducts heat.
