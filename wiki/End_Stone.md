# End Stone

A vanilla block. Feedback gives it two further uses: a source of hydrogen and helium, and a low-grade tungsten ore.

## What vanilla does

A pale, hard block found on the End island. It is a building block.

## What Feedback does

End stone heated in a [Reactor](Reactor.md#the-reactions) at **700 to 1300 Tu** releases **250 mB of hydrogen** and **100 mB of [helium](Chemicals.md#helium)** and leaves **Porous Aggregate**, a very light rock that barely conducts heat.

End stone is also a mineral that carries a little tungsten, so it takes the [ore ladder](Ore_Processing.md). Hammer a block of it to Crushed End Stone ([Mechanical Hammer](Mechanical_Hammer.md#crushing-ore) or by hand on an [Anvil](Anvil.md)), then wash it in the [Mixer](Mixer.md#washing-ore), draw it with an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md#separating-ore) or sort it with a [Belt Optical Reader](Belt_Modules.md#belt-optical-reader) for End Stone Concentrate and Tailings. In the [Reactor](Reactor.md) at **150 to 250 Tu**, 200 mB of lye leaches the concentrate into **33 mB** of sodium tungstate and the tailings into **10 mB**. Tungsten is precipitated from the liquor as in [Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md#leaching-and-winning).
