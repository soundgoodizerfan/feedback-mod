# Iron

Iron is the elemental metal, shown in the game as **Pure Iron**: magnetic, soft, ductile and tough, and the default part metal of many modular items. It is one rung of the iron ladder: cast iron (the vanilla Iron Ingot), [wrought iron](Materials.md#the-metals), pure iron and [steel](Steel.md). It belongs to the Iron [age](Ages.md).

## Getting it

- **Ore.** Iron Ore lies in the Overworld at y 80 to 384, y -24 to 56 and y -64 to 72. Hematite, limonite and magnetite are iron minerals ([Ores](Ores.md)).
- **Smelting.** Raw iron, crushed ore, concentrate or dust with charcoal in a crucible between **1420 and 1650 Tu** gives an **Iron Ingot**, which is [cast iron](Materials.md#the-metals): hard and brittle. Held lower, the ore becomes a slag-filled bloom that is hammered into wrought iron ([Guide: Making Wrought Iron](Guide_Making_Wrought_Iron.md)). Roasting pyrite and sulfide concentrates leaves cast iron nuggets, the vanilla Iron Nugget ([Ore Processing](Ore_Processing.md)).
- **Reducing ore.** Raw iron, crushed ore, concentrate or dust with **450 mB** of [hydrogen](Chemicals.md#hydrogen) in the [Reactor](Reactor.md), held between **500 and 1000 Tu** (best near 700), gives a **Pure Iron Ingot**. The ore stays solid, and no carbon or slag is involved.
- **Decarburizing cast iron.** An Iron Ingot (cast iron) with **300 mB** of hydrogen between **800 and 1100 Tu** (best near 950) gives a Pure Iron Ingot. It is slow, and above **1150 Tu** the batch spoils into Burnt Iron.
- **Electrowinning.** Leach cast iron nuggets, swarf or dust in **150 mB** of sulfuric acid at **40 to 110 Tu** in the Reactor. That gives **250 mB** of **Iron Sulfate Liquor**, 100 mB of hydrogen, and graphite dust (the carbon of the cast iron) left behind. The [Electrolyser](Electrolyser.md#plated-metal) plates the liquor into Pure Iron Nuggets and vents oxygen.
- **Items.** Pure Iron Ingot, Nugget and Block craft into each other like any metal, and are separate from the vanilla Iron Ingot, Nugget and Block, which are cast iron.
- **Working window.** Iron only moves under the hammer between **900 and 1500 Tu** ([Metalworking](Metalworking.md)).

## Figures

| Figure | Iron |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness (St) | 8 |
| Density (Mu per mB) | 7.87 |
| Melting point (Tu) | 1538 |
| Toughness | 80 |
| Yield strength (St) | 7.5 |
| Expansion (millionths per Tu) | 11.8 |
| Heat conduction | 0.02 |
| Conductivity | 0.17 |
| Corrosion resistance | 0.37 |

Iron is **magnetic** until it passes 770 Tu, so an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) pulls it. It rusts: its corrosion resistance is the lowest of the common metals here, and an iron plate in an acid wears fast ([Battery](Battery.md#the-electrolyte)). An iron edge mines iron ore and gold ore, not obsidian.

## What it is used in

- **Shafts.** Iron's yield strength just reaches the 7.5 St that [Shafts](Shaft.md) ask of an ingot.
- **Batteries.** Iron is the anode of the iron and nickel cell, with [nickel](Nickel.md) as the cathode ([Battery](Battery.md)).
- **Electrolysis.** A base metal behind lead and tin in the [Electrolytic Cell](Electrolytic_Cell.md#the-liquid-picks-the-metal).
- **Fuse wire.** Blows above about 5.9 A ([Fuse](Fuse.md)).
- **Guns.** An iron chamber rates 5 Pu for a 9 mm bore and 3 mm wall ([Gun Parts](Gun_Parts.md#the-chamber)).
- **Alloys.** [Steel](Steel.md) is iron with carbon; invar and alnico take iron ([Casting](Casting.md#alloys)).
- **Fasteners.** Quality 2 ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)).

See [Metals](Metals.md) for the other metals.
