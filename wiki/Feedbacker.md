# Feedbacker

A gauntlet that **punches any projectile back** at whoever sent it, faster than it came. The name is the mod's: a machine that returns what you give it.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, and it grants the technique itself: no skill needed.

## Getting one

Craft it:

```
P C P
P E P
_ L _
```

P is a Tempered Steel Plate, C a Copper Coil, E an Electromagnet and L Leather. The recipe appears once you have tempered steel plate.

It is also a **rare find** (about 4%) in dungeon, stronghold and ancient city chests, already 30 to 70% worn.

## Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot, and press **G** (parry) with **nothing in your hand**. A window opens (12 ticks, a 75° cone, 20 ticks of recovery).

- **Any projectile** (arrow, bolt, thrown weapon) that reaches you in the window is turned and sent back along your look at **2.5×** their speed.
- **A ghast fireball** comes back as well, without exploding on you. It costs much more of the gauntlet.
- **Bullets** from a [gun](Guns.md): if you punch as a shot is fired, facing the shooter, it is negated and a return shot hits the shooter for **2.5× the bullet's damage**, credited to you. A punched-back shot can't be parried again. (A *weapon* parry doesn't catch bullets: they land.)

It plays its own sound, and the arm you punch with is drawn with the gauntlet's skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.

## What it costs

A punch takes **1 wear plus 12 for every whole multiple of the gauntlet's rating** (150 impulse, raised by the worn gear's joint share) the projectile carried. An arrow is about 45; a ghast fireball is 120 to 150; anything much heavier is far over. A punch that is over the rating wrecks it for 150 wear.

It has 400 durability, and **it is never destroyed**: at its last point it simply stops granting the punch. There is no way to repair it yet.
