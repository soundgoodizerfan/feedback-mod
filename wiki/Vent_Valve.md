# Vent Valve

Lets lift gas out of the [Gas Cells](Gas_Cell.md) beside it, to come down or to relieve a full cell before a climb.

## Crafting

```
P B P
  L
```

P is a steel plate, B is iron bars and L is a lever.

## Using it

- **Right-click** to open or shut it by hand. The message says *Vent open* or *Vent shut*.
- A [Controller](Controller.md) card can drive it through the analog channel **Vent**; the card's level sets how far it opens, and the gas leaves faster the wider it is open.

It drains the cells beside it, and cells that touch share gas, so one valve drains a whole block of cells.
