# Survey Meter

Tells you what ore, and what oil, lies around you, so you do not dig blind. Read-only, needs no power, and unlocks nothing.

## Crafting

```
  R
I S I
  S
```

R is Redstone Dust, I is a Wrought Iron Plate and S is a [Shaft](Shaft.md).

## Using it

Right click. It lists up to four kinds of ore within 16 blocks, each with a rough block count and which way most of it lies. It then needs a couple of seconds before the next reading.

The count is rounded to the nearest 8 and wobbles a little each time, so a thin vein may read as nothing and a fat one will not read the same twice. It is a guide, not a count.

## Oil

An **oil basin** reads as a line of its own, from how close the nearest basin's circle comes, with a bearing towards its centre. Same rounding, same wobble. See [Crude Oil](Crude_Oil.md#finding-a-basin).

## Outcrops

Some veins show themselves: a lone block of the ore sitting on the surface above a vein that is not buried too deep. Tin, zinc, lead, copper, iron and graphite do this. Follow it down.
