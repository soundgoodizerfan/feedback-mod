# Ballast Tank

A tank of water that weighs an [airship](Airship.md) down. Dumping the water lightens the craft so it rises.

## Crafting

```
P P P
P B P
P P P
```

P is a steel plate and B is a bucket.

## Using it

- The tank holds **4000 mB** of water. Fill it with a bucket or a [Pipe](Pipe.md).
- **Right-click** with an empty hand to switch dumping on or off. The message says *Dumping ballast* or *Holding ballast* with the amount aboard.
- A [Controller](Controller.md) card can drive it through the analog channel **Ballast dump**.
- With [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) the tank reads **No ballast**, **Little ballast**, **Half ballasted** or **Heavily ballasted**.

The water weighs what water weighs, so a full tank is a large share of a small ship. Ballast trims the craft too, according to where the tank sits.
