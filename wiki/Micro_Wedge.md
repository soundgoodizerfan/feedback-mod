# Micro Wedge

A four-step ramp for [micro builds](Micro_Core.md). As a voxel it is a ramp a quarter of a block long, falling away from you.

## Crafting

```
S 
SS
```

S is a Smooth Stone Slab. The recipe makes 2.

## Using it

Hold it and right-click a [micro cell](Micro_Core.md) as you would any block. The ramp falls away from you. Click the upper half of a face to hang it from above instead.

Each of the four steps is 1/16 of a block. What you see is what collides and what the outline shows: the same four steps. The wedge has a single look.
