# Silicon

A dark, brittle metalloid. It casts like a metal but breaks like glass, and it is for glass and optics.

## Two grades

- **Crude silicon**: sand with charcoal at 1600 to 1800 Tu.
- **Silicon**: nether quartz with charcoal at 1600 to 1800 Tu. Purer.

Both melt at 1414 Tu and cast into ingots. Plates and rods are worked from the ingot like a metal's, with a hard enough hammer.

## Glass

Melt sand with a silicon ingot in a crucible at 1500 Tu or more and it makes glass. Crude silicon makes **silica glass**; pure silicon makes **optical glass**, the clearest there is. Both cast into ingots; plates and rods are worked from them. See [Optics](Optics.md).
