## 4. Animation system (the big one)

### What exists (all unplayed since the last edits; `slice` and the first swing clip worked early on)
- **playerAnimator 2.0.4** (KosmX, MIT) is jarJar'd. Maven `https://maven.kosmx.dev/`, property `player_anim_version`. Reference clone at `../PlayerAnimator-reference` (1.21 branch).
- **Better Combat 2.4.0** is now a **required mod, client side** (`neoforge.mods.toml`, `compileOnly` + `devMods` in `build.gradle`, pinned with Cloth Config in `build-and-deploy.sh`). Licence: all rights reserved. Nothing of it is copied or shipped. Reference clone at `../BetterCombat-reference`. We call its `PlayerAttackAnimatable.playAttackAnimation(...)`, play its clips and sounds by id, and use none of its attack rules.
- `client/UpswingSwing`: left click on a modular blade, mace, spear or a bare hand is cancelled, the Better Combat clip starts with its own upswing gearing, and vanilla's attack (or our swept volume) fires when the clip strikes. Relayed to onlookers by `net/SwingStartPayload` (client to server) and `SwingPlayPayload` (server to others). Upswing and length come from the weapon's baked heaviness and attack delay. Holding the click never repeats (the author hates hold-to-attack).
- `combat/hit/Wielding`: two-handed = spear always, blade over heaviness 0.7, mace over 0.6; a two-hander in either hand shuts the off hand. Dual wielding = a one-handed strike weapon in each hand: **left click is always the main hand, right click always the off hand**, never alternating. `DualWieldBoost` adds x1.2 attack speed; `OffhandStrike.cooldownTicks` follows it.
- `client/WeaponPose`: idle holds (`pose_two_handed_sword/heavy/polearm/bow/crossbow`) on two layers, chosen per client from the held stack.
- `client/SwingClips`: our own layer for the roll (`fist_tumble_right`, the roll's only pose now), the spear's held charge and throw, and the Feedbacker parry punch (Better Combat's `one_handed_punch`). It also logs a warning and sends one chat line per session telling players to report problems to Feedback, never to Better Combat's author (`feedback.better_combat_notice`).
- `CurioRenderers.FirstPersonSkinLayer`: draws the Feedbacker's skin arm in the first-person pass, because the library drops every layer except item-in-hand ones there.
- The procedural roll in `CombatPose` was deleted by the author's ruling. The parry, kick and stagger poses there remain.

## 5. Rulings made this session (also worth carrying forward)
- Breaking changes stay fine (CLAUDE.md); no migrations.
- Better Combat is a required dependency; our code owns attack rules; its rendering is used.
- Left click is the main hand, right click the off hand, always.
- No hold-to-attack, no alternation.
- Roll is only the animation; no procedural roll tuck.
- Feedbacker punch must feel instant; the sound must not lag.
- Parry window much tighter.

## 6. Traps found
- `cp` is aliased interactive in this shell and hangs a background job even with `-f`; copy files with Python (`shutil.copyfile`) or `command cp`.
- Mid-turn the harness reports files "changed on disk"; that was my own edits, not the user's.
- A new Java file's licence header must end with ` */` before `package`; copying 18 lines of the header cut it short once.
- `FormItems` reads material files raw (no parent resolution): any material that gets its `kind` by `parent` generates no forms.
- `ParryStats`, `CombatPose` and the new animation classes all touch the parry; change the window in `ParryTuning` only.
